BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Marvell Technology designs and sells semiconductor chips that power data‑center networking, cloud storage, and 5G infrastructure. Its products are embedded in the silicon of major cloud providers, telecom carriers, and enterprise networking gear, giving it a direct pipeline to the fastest‑growing part of the tech economy. The moat comes from deep integration with a handful of tier‑1 customers and a portfolio of patented high‑speed interconnect IP, which makes switching to a rival costly because redesigning data‑center boards can take months and billions of dollars.

Marvell Technology 設計與銷售用於資料中心網路、雲端儲存與 5G 基礎建設的半導體晶片。其產品嵌入主要雲端服務商、電信營運商與企業網路設備，直接把握科技產業增長最快的環節。護城河在於與少數頂級客戶的深度整合以及一系列高頻寬互連技術的專利，換供應商需要重新設計資料中心板卡，耗時數月、成本數十億美元，門檻極高。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Marvell posted Q4 FY2025 revenue of $2.07 billion, a 22% year‑over‑year increase, driven by strong demand for its Ethernet switch and custom ASIC solutions. The company also announced a $1.5 billion investment in a new fab partnership to secure advanced‑node production for AI‑focused chips, expanding its addressable market beyond networking.
