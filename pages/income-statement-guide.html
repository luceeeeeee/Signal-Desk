01

Revenue  /  營收

Total Sales · 總銷售額
Plain Language / 白話說明
Revenue is the total amount of money collected from customers before paying for anything — product sales, subscriptions, services, every dollar in the door.

營收是公司向客戶收取的所有款項總和，尚未扣除任何費用。包含商品銷售、訂閱費、服務費——所有進帳金額。
Formula / 公式
Revenue = Units Sold × Price Per Unit
(or sum of all income streams)

營收 = 銷售數量 × 單價
（或所有收入來源加總）
Real Example — Apple FY2024 / 真實案例
Total Revenue $391B
Apple collected $391 billion — from iPhones ($201B), Services ($96B), Macs, iPads, and Wearables. Revenue grew 2% vs. FY2023. This tells you the company is still growing, but slowly.

蘋果收入3,910億美元——來自iPhone（2,010億）、服務（960億）、Mac、iPad及穿戴裝置。營收年增2%，公司仍在成長，但速度偏慢。
Why It Matters / 為什麼重要
  • 📏
    Size signal: Revenue tells you how big the business actually is. $400B and $4B are fundamentally different companies.規模指標：營收告訴你這間公司到底有多大。3,940億美元和40億美元是完全不同等級的公司。
  • 📈
    Growth rate matters more than absolute size: 20% revenue growth often excites investors more than a large but flat revenue base.成長率比絕對數字更重要：20%的營收成長往往比一個龐大但停滯的營收基礎更令投資人興奮。
  • ⚠️
    Revenue alone doesn't mean profit: A company can generate $1B in revenue and still lose money. Always read revenue alongside margin numbers.光有營收不代表有利潤：一間公司可以有10億美元的營收卻仍然虧損。務必同時看利潤率數字。
02

Gross Margin  /  毛利率

Profit after production costs · 扣除生產成本後的利潤
Plain Language / 白話說明
Gross margin is how much money is left after paying to make or deliver the product — just the direct cost of producing what was sold. Excludes salaries, offices, everything else.

毛利率是扣除生產或交付產品的直接成本後剩下的錢——只算製造銷售出去的東西所需的直接費用，不含薪資及辦公室等。
Formula / 公式
Gross Margin % =
(Revenue − Cost of Goods Sold)
──────────────────────────── × 100
Revenue

毛利率 = (營收 − 銷貨成本) ÷ 營收 × 100
Real Example — Apple FY2024 / 真實案例
Gross Margin 46%
For every $100 Apple collected, $54 went to components, manufacturing, and delivery. $46 remained. Compare: Spotify ~27%, NVIDIA 70%+. Higher gross margin = stronger pricing power and production efficiency.

蘋果每收100元，54元用於零組件、製造與配送，剩下46元。對比：Spotify約27%，NVIDIA超過70%。毛利率愈高，代表定價能力愈強、生產效率愈高。
Why It Matters / 為什麼重要
  • 🏰
    Moat indicator: Companies with consistent 60%+ gross margins usually have a strong competitive advantage — customers can't easily switch to something cheaper.護城河指標：持續維持60%以上毛利率的公司，通常擁有強大的競爭優勢——客戶不容易轉換到更便宜的替代品。
  • 📉
    Shrinking margin is a warning: If gross margin falls quarter over quarter, the company is likely cutting prices to compete, or input costs are rising faster than it can pass on.毛利率下滑是警訊：若毛利率連續幾季下降，通常代表公司正在降價競爭，或原物料成本上漲速度超過其轉嫁能力。
  • 🔄
    Compare within the same industry: 20% is excellent for a grocery chain, mediocre for a software company. Always compare apples to apples.同業比較才有意義：20%的毛利率對超市連鎖業者而言非常優秀，但對軟體公司卻算普通。比較時務必在同一個產業內比。
03

Operating Margin  /  營業利益率

Profit after running the full business · 扣除所有日常運營費用後的利潤
Plain Language / 白話說明
Operating margin is what's left after also paying for salaries, offices, R&D, and marketing — the full cost of running the business day to day, not just making the product.

營業利益率是還要再扣掉薪資、辦公室、研發及行銷費用後的剩餘——日常經營整個公司的成本，不只是製造產品而已。
Formula / 公式
Operating Margin % =
Operating Income
─────────────── × 100
Revenue

營業利益率 = 營業利益 ÷ 營收 × 100
Real Example — Apple FY2024 / 真實案例
Operating Margin 31%
After paying ~$120B in operating expenses (R&D, retail, marketing, staff), Apple's operating income was ~$120B on $391B in revenue. For every $100 in sales, $31 remained after running the entire company. Most large companies operate at 10–20%.

支付約1,200億美元的營業費用（研發、零售、行銷、員工）後，每100元銷售中有31元留下。多數大公司的營業利益率僅10–20%。
Why It Matters / 為什麼重要
  • ⚖️
    Operating leverage: When revenue grows but expenses stay flat, operating margin expands — every new dollar of revenue drops almost directly to profit. This is the "scale effect."營運槓桿效應：當營收成長但費用維持不變，營業利益率就會擴大——每多一元的收入幾乎直接轉化為利潤。這就是所謂的「規模效應」。
  • 🔍
    More complete than gross margin: A company can have a high gross margin but still burn cash if it overspends on marketing or R&D. Operating margin reveals whether management is disciplined.比毛利率更全面：一間公司可以有很高的毛利率，但若行銷或研發支出過度，仍然會燒錢。營業利益率揭示管理層是否有節制地控制費用。
04

Net Margin  /  淨利率

Final profit — after everything · 扣除所有項目後的最終利潤
Plain Language / 白話說明
Net margin is the final profit — after paying everything including interest on debt and income taxes. It's the number that belongs to shareholders. Everything above is a stepping stone to get here.

淨利率是最終利潤——扣除所有費用包含債務利息及所得稅之後的餘額。這個數字屬於股東。上方所有行都是為了算出這個數字。
Formula / 公式
Net Margin % =
Net Income
─────────── × 100
Revenue

淨利率 = 淨利潤 ÷ 營收 × 100
Real Example — Apple FY2024 / 真實案例
Net Margin 26%
Apple's net income was ~$101B on $391B revenue. For every $100 collected, $26 was pure profit. For context: average S&P 500 company nets ~$10. A supermarket like Walmart nets $2–3. Apple's 26% is elite-tier.

蘋果淨利約1,010億美元。每100元收入中，有26元是純利潤。標普500平均約10元，沃爾瑪僅2–3元。蘋果的26%屬於頂尖水準。
Why It Matters / 為什麼重要
  • 💰
    This is what investors actually own: Net income flows to shareholders via buybacks or dividends, or gets reinvested for future growth.這才是投資人真正擁有的：淨利潤透過回購或股息流向股東，或重新投入以支持未來成長。
  • 📊
    Benchmark by sector: Software: 20–40% is normal. Retail: 2–5%. Banking: 15–25%. Pharma: 15–30%. Never judge a margin without knowing the industry.按產業對標：軟體業20–40%屬正常；零售業2–5%；銀行業15–25%；製藥業15–30%。判斷利潤率前，務必先了解所在產業。
  • 🧨
    One-time items can distort: A company might show high net income from an asset sale — not real earnings. Always check if the profit came from ongoing operations.一次性項目會扭曲數字：公司可能因出售資產而顯示高淨利——但那不是真實的營業利潤。務必確認利潤是否來自持續性的核心業務。
05

EPS — Earnings Per Share  /  每股盈餘

How much each share earned · 每持有一股可賺取多少利潤
Plain Language / 白話說明
EPS answers: "If I own one share, how much of the company's profit do I own?" Total profit divided by total shares — makes it possible to compare companies of wildly different sizes.

EPS回答：「如果我持有一股，我擁有多少利潤？」總利潤除以股份數量——讓你能比較規模差異極大的公司。

若分析師預期6.43美元而蘋果交出6.57美元，稱為「超越預期」——股價通常上漲。若僅6.30美元，稱為「不及預期」——股價通常下跌。超越或不及預期往往比EPS絕對數字更重要。
Why It Matters / 為什麼重要
  • 🔢
    The most-watched number on earnings day: Analysts set an EPS estimate before results. The entire market reacts to whether the company beat or missed that estimate.財報日最受關注的數字：分析師在業績公布前設定EPS預期。整個市場的反應取決於公司是否超越或不及這個預期。
  • 📐
    Enables fair comparison: NVIDIA and a small chipmaker both report EPS. The size of each company doesn't matter — you compare what each single share delivers.讓比較變得公平：NVIDIA和一家小型晶片商都用EPS報告。公司規模不重要——你比較的是每一股所帶來的盈利能力。
  • 📉
    P/E ratio is built on EPS: P/E = Stock Price ÷ EPS. If EPS rises, P/E falls — the stock becomes relatively cheaper even if the price stays flat.本益比建立在EPS之上：本益比 = 股價 ÷ EPS。若EPS上升，本益比下降——即使股價不變，股票也變得相對便宜。

Quick Reference / 速查表

Beat / 超越預期
Actual results came in higher than what analysts predicted. Usually positive for the stock price.
實際業績高於分析師預測。通常對股價有正面影響。
Miss / 不及預期
Actual results came in lower than analyst estimates. Usually negative for the stock price.
實際業績低於分析師預期。通常對股價有負面影響。
YoY / 年增率
Year-over-year: compares this quarter to the same quarter last year. Removes seasonal effects.
年對年比較：將本季與去年同季相比。消除季節性影響，反映真實成長趨勢。
QoQ / 季增率
Quarter-over-quarter: compares this quarter to the previous quarter. Shows short-term momentum.
季對季比較：將本季與上一季相比。顯示短期動能與近期趨勢變化。
Guidance / 業績展望
Management's forecast for the next quarter or year. Often moves the stock more than current results.
管理層對下一季或下一年度的業績預測。往往比當前業績更能左右股價走勢。
Consensus / 市場共識
Average of all analyst estimates. "Beating consensus" means beating this average.
所有分析師預測的平均值。「超越市場共識」即表示業績高於此平均預期。
P/E Ratio / 本益比
Stock price ÷ EPS. How much investors pay per $1 of earnings. Lower = relatively cheaper.
股價 ÷ EPS。投資人為每1美元盈利所付的價格。數字越低，股票相對越便宜。
Diluted Shares / 稀釋後股數
Total shares including stock options and convertibles — the conservative EPS denominator.
包含股票選擇權及可轉換債券的總股數——計算EPS時採用的保守分母。
