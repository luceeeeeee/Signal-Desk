BUSINESS OVERVIEW

TSM (Taiwan Semiconductor Manufacturing Company) is the world’s biggest factory that makes chips for other companies. It does not design its own products; instead it manufactures silicon wafers for customers such as Apple, Nvidia and Qualcomm. Its moat is very strong because it controls roughly 55 % of the global advanced‑node (≤7 nm) market, and the equipment and expertise required to produce at these tiny geometries costs tens of billions of dollars. This creates high switching costs – once a customer qualifies a fab, moving to a rival would mean months of re‑qualification and massive expense.

營運概況

TSM（台積電）是全球最大的代工晶圓廠，專門為其他公司製造晶片，客戶包括 Apple、Nvidia、Qualcomm 等。它的護城河極為堅固，因為它佔全球先進製程（≤7 nm）約 55 % 的市場份額，建造與維護此等先進製程的設備與技術成本高達數百億美元。這讓客戶一旦驗證完成，換廠將需耗費數月時間與巨額費用，形成極高的切換成本。

---

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months TSM’s revenue surged 35.1 % YoY, driven by record demand for high‑performance computing chips and a rebound in smartphone sales. The company announced a $40 billion investment to expand capacity in its 3‑nm fab, targeting volume production by Q4 2025.

最近發展

過去六個月，TSM 營收同比成長 35.1 %，主要受高效能運算晶片需求創新高以及手機銷售回暖推動。公司同時宣佈將在 3 奈米廠房再投資 400億美元，目標於 2025 年第四季開始量產。

---

CATALYSTS

  • 3‑nm volume ramp: Full‑scale production of 3‑nm chips is expected to begin in Q4 2025, unlocking higher‑margin orders from Nvidia and Apple. The incremental capacity could add $15 billion of revenue annually.
  • Geopolitical supply‑chain shift: U.S. and EU policies encouraging on‑shoring of semiconductor supply are prompting customers to secure capacity with trusted founders; TSM stands to capture a larger share of those “friend‑shoring” contracts.
  • Advanced packaging breakthrough: Successful rollout of TSM’s CoWoS and InFO advanced‑package technologies could boost average selling price by 5‑7 % as customers integrate more functions per chip.

催化劑

  • 3 奈米量產：2025 年第四季起全面投產 3 奈米晶片，將為 Nvidia、Apple 等高毛利訂單解鎖。預計每年可額外帶來 150億美元 營收。
  • 地緣政治供應鏈轉移：美國與歐盟鼓勵本土半導體供應，客戶會優先向可信賴的代工廠購買，TSM 有望贏得更多「友好外包」合約。
  • 先進封裝突破：TSM 的 CoWoS 與 InFO 先進封裝技術若成功推廣，平均售價可提升 5‑7 %，因為客戶可在單顆晶片內整合更多功能。

---

RISKS

  • U.S.–China tech decoupling: If the U.S. imposes tighter export restrictions on advanced‑node equipment, TSM’s ability to expand 3‑nm capacity could be delayed, shaving up to 10 % off projected revenue growth.
  • Demand slowdown in AI GPUs: A softer-than‑expected AI server market would reduce Nvidia’s orders, cutting TSM’s earnings by an estimated $3 billion in a year.
  • Currency headwinds: A strong New Taiwan dollar against the dollar would erode export margins, potentially lowering net profit margin by 0.5 percentage points.

風險

  • 美中科技脫鉤：若美國進一步限制先進製程設備出口，TSM 的 3 奈米產能擴張將受阻，營收成長可能減少 10 %。
  • AI GPU 需求下降：若 AI 伺服器市場不如預期，Nvidia 的訂單減少，年內可能削減 TSM 約 30億美元 的盈餘。
  • 匯率壓力：新臺幣兌美元升值會侵蝕出口毛利率，淨利率可能下降 0.5 個百分點。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s level of $396 because the forward P/E of 20.5 is well below the 5‑year average of ~28, giving a clear valuation cushion. The upside to $460 (≈ +16 %) is supported by the 3‑nm ramp and AI demand, while a drop below $340 would force a re‑evaluation. I see a strong moat lasting at least the next five years thanks to scale, tech lead, and entrenched customer relationships.

結論

以目前 396 美元 的價格建議買入，因為 20.5 倍的預期本益比遠低於過去五年平均約 28 倍，提供明顯估值安全邊際。若 3 奈米產能全面開工且 AI 需求持續，股價有望升至 460 美元（約 +16 %），若跌破 340 美元 則須重新評估。從規模、技術領先與客戶黏著度來看，未來五年護城河仍然堅固。
