BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Intuitive Surgical makes and sells the da Vinci surgical robot, a system that lets surgeons perform minimally‑invasive operations with computer‑assisted precision. The company controls roughly 80% of the global market for robotic‑assisted surgery, and hospitals that buy the system face high switching costs because surgeons must train for years to become proficient and the robots are deeply integrated into operating‑room workflows. The brand enjoys a premium price—about 40% higher than the nearest competitor—thanks to proven clinical outcomes and extensive data that reinforce its reputation.

业务概览

Intuitive Surgical 生产并销售达芬奇手术机器人，这套系统让外科医生以电脑辅助手段进行微创手术。公司约占全球机器人辅助手术市场 80% 的份额，医院购买后换厂商成本很高，因为外科医生需要多年培训才能熟练使用，且机器人已深度嵌入手术室流程。品牌还能收取约比最近竞争者高出 40% 的溢价，得益于可靠的临床成果和大量数据支撑其声誉。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the latest quarter (Mar ’26) revenue reached $2.77 billion, a 23% year‑over‑year increase, while net income rose to $0.82 billion, confirming the company’s ability to grow both top‑line and profit despite a slight dip in unit shipments. The firm also announced a new generation of the da Vinci system slated for commercial launch in Q4 2026, promising faster set‑up times and lower per‑procedure cost.

最近发展
