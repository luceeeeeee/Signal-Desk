BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English: Lowe’s Companies operates a chain of home‑improvement stores across the United States, offering building materials, appliances, tools and garden products. It holds roughly 15% of the U.S. home‑improvement market, second only to Home Depot. The moat comes from its massive store network, a well‑known “DIY” brand that draws frequent repeat visits, and a logistics system that delivers large, bulky items faster than most regional rivals. Switching costs are high because most homeowners prefer a single trusted retailer for the whole renovation project.
