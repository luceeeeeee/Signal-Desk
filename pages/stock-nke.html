BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Nike designs, markets and sells athletic footwear, apparel and equipment worldwide, most of its sales coming from its own branded stores and e‑commerce sites. It commands roughly 30% of the global athletic‑shoe market and enjoys a premium pricing advantage—its shoes sell for about 20‑40% more than most generic brands because consumers associate the “Swoosh” with style, performance and cultural cachet. The moat rests on its powerful brand, massive global distribution network, and deep data on consumer preferences that create high switching costs; a newcomer would need billions in marketing spend just to gain a fraction of Nike’s loyalty.

企業概況

Nike在全球設計、行銷與販售運動鞋、服飾與相關裝備，主要營收來自自有品牌門市與電商。它占全球運動鞋市場大約30%的份額，且因「Swoosh」品牌形象，產品價格比一般品牌高出20‑40%。護城河來自品牌力度、遍佈全球的通路網以及對消費者喜好的大量數據，讓顧客換品牌成本很高；新進者要獲得一點市場佔有率，必須投入數十億美元的行銷費用。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Nike announced a $1.3 billion investment in its direct‑to‑consumer (DTC) platform, accelerating store openings in key growth markets and expanding its digital app ecosystem. The company reported FY2025 revenue of $46.7 billion, up only 0.1% year‑over‑year, while earnings fell 34.8% as wholesale partner margins compressed.

最近動向

過去六個月，Nike宣佈投入13億美元加速自營通路（DTC），包括在成長快速的市場開新店，以及擴大其數位APP生態系。公司公布2025財年營收467億美元，年增僅0.1%，但因批發合作夥伴毛利率縮減，盈餘下跌34.8%。

CATALYSTS

  • DTC Expansion: Continued rollout of Nike-owned stores and online channels should lift gross margins by 150‑200 basis points per year as the company captures more of the retail markup. Expect incremental contribution starting FY2026 (July 2025 ET).
  • Product Innovation Cycle: Launch of the next‑generation “Air Max” and “ZoomX” running shoes slated for Q3 2025 (Sept 2025 TPE) could drive a 3‑5% sales boost in the high‑margin performance segment.
  • Supply‑Chain Cost Improvements: A multi‑year agreement with major raw‑material suppliers aims to lock in lower rubber and foam prices, potentially reducing cost of goods sold by 0.5% annually beginning FY2026.

催化劑

  • 【自營通路擴張】：持續開設Nike自有門市與線上平台，將每年提升毛利率150‑200個基點，因公司可以直接取得零售加價的收益。預計從2026財年（2025年7月美東時間）開始貢獻。
  • 【產品創新週期】：預計於2025年第3季（2025年9月台北時間）推出下一代「Air Max」與「ZoomX」跑鞋，可能讓高毛利績效類別營收提升3‑5%。
  • 【供應鏈成本改善】：與主要原料供應商簽訂多年度合約，鎖定較低的橡膠與泡棉價格，預計從2026財年起每年降低製造成本0.5%。

---

RISKS

  • Wholesale Dependence: If key retail partners (e.g., Foot Locker, JD Sports) cut orders due to inventory excess, Nike’s revenue could drop 2‑3% in the next 12 months, compressing margins further.
  • Consumer Sentiment Shift: A prolonged economic slowdown could reduce discretionary spending on premium footwear, risking a revenue decline of 4‑5% YoY and forcing deeper discounting.
  • Geopolitical Tension: Escalation of US‑China trade frictions could trigger new tariffs on Chinese‑made shoes, raising cost of goods sold by up to 1% and hurting profitability.

風險

  • 【對批發的依賴】：若主要零售合作夥伴（如Foot Locker、JD Sports）因庫存過多而削減訂單，Nike未來12個月營收可能下降2‑3%，毛利率進一步壓縮。
  • 【消費者情緒變化】：若經濟持續疲弱，消費者在高價位運動鞋上的支出減少，可能導致營收年減4‑5%，迫使公司更深度打折。
  • 【地緣政治緊張】：美中貿易摩擦升級可能對中國製造的鞋子加徵新關稅，將製造成本提升最高1%，侵蝕獲利。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s $44.36 price. The forward P/E of 24× is below the 5‑year historical average of ~27×, giving a modest valuation cushion, while the brand moat remains strong and DTC investments are beginning to lift margins. A break below $40 would signal that the DTC rollout is not delivering, prompting a reassessment. Over the next five years the brand’s cultural relevance and data‑driven design should keep the moat durable.

結論

以目前44.36美元的股價建議買進。24倍的預估本益比低於過去五年約27倍的平均水平，提供一定的估值緩衝；同時品牌護城河仍然穩固，自營通路的投資已開始提升毛利。若股價跌破40美元，表示自營通路效益未如預期，需重新評估。未來五年內，品牌的文化影響力與數據驅動的設計將保持護城河的耐久性。
