BUSINESS OVERVIEW

JP Morgan Chase is the world’s biggest universal bank, offering consumer banking, credit cards, wealth management, corporate lending, and investment‑banking services. Its sheer size gives it a massive “network effect” – customers and corporations keep their money and financing relationships with one bank because switching would require moving billions of dollars and breaking long‑standing contracts. The firm controls roughly 12 % of U.S. banking assets and enjoys a premium brand that lets it charge higher fees on corporate deals. This combination of scale, deep relationships and brand makes a durable moat that most new entrants cannot match.

[來源 | 地區] JPMorgan Chase 企業概況

JP Morgan Chase 是全球最大的全方位銀行，提供個人銀行、信用卡、財富管理、企業貸款以及投資銀行服務。 其龐大的規模產生「網路效應」——客戶和公司會把數十億美元的資金與融資關係留在同一家銀行，因為轉換成本極高且涉及長期合約。 該公司約佔美國銀行資產的 12%，品牌優勢使其在企業交易上能收取較高的費用。 這種規模、深厚關係和品牌組合構成了大多數新進者難以超越的護城河。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the past six months JPM reported a 12.7 % rise in total revenue, driven by a 15 % jump in consumer‑banking deposits and a 9 % increase in investment‑banking fees. Net income climbed 13 % to $13.03 billion in the December quarter, while the balance‑sheet showed cash of $1.86 trillion versus debt of $1.31 trillion, a 1.4‑to‑1 coverage ratio.

過去六個月 JPM 營收成長 12.7%，主要因為消費者銀行存款增加 15% 以及投資銀行手續費提升 9%。 12月季的淨利增至 130.3 億美元，增幅 13%。 同時資產負債表顯示現金 1.864 兆美元，負債 1.311 兆美元，現金對負債比為 1.4：1。

  • Interest‑Rate Cycle: As the Fed keeps rates near the top of the cycle, net interest margin – the profit made on loans versus deposits – stays high. Expect a 0.3‑percentage‑point boost to earnings each quarter until rates start to fall.
