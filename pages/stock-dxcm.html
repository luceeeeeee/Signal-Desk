BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Dexcom makes continuous‑glucose‑monitor (CGM) devices that people with diabetes wear on their skin to see their blood‑sugar levels in real time. The company supplies the hardware, a mobile app, and a subscription‑based cloud service that stores data and alerts users when glucose is out of range. Dexcom controls roughly 35% of the U.S. CGM market and enjoys a strong network effect: doctors who have prescribed the system tend to keep using it because patients become accustomed to the data stream, making it costly for patients to switch to a competitor’s product. The brand also commands a price premium of about 30% over lower‑cost alternatives, which helps protect margins.

Dexcom 於美國提供穿戴式血糖即時監測裝置，讓糖尿病患者可以即時看到血糖變化。公司同時提供手機應用程式與雲端資料服務，並收取訂閱費。它佔美國約 35% 的 CGM 市場，因醫師習慣開立其裝置、患者已習慣使用資料平台，形成高切換成本的網路效應。品牌亦能比低價競品收取約 30% 的價格溢價，保護毛利。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Dexcom reported a 15% year‑over‑year revenue increase, reaching $1.26 billion in the latest quarter, and announced FDA clearance for its next‑generation CGM sensor that promises a 10‑day wear life. The company also secured a new partnership with a major health‑system insurer covering its CGM for 1 million additional members, which should add roughly $150 million of recurring revenue in 2025.

過去六個月 Dexcom 營收年增 15%，最新季營收達 12.6 億美元，並取得 FDA 核准的次世代 CGM 感測器，使用壽命可延長至 10 天。公司同時與一家大型健康保險系統達成合作，為 100 萬名新增會員提供 CGM，預估 2025 年將帶來約 1.5 億美元的持續收入。

CATALYSTS

  • New Sensor Launch: FDA clearance for the 10‑day sensor is expected in Q4 2024 (ET) / 2025年第一季 (TPE). The longer wear time reduces replacement cost for users, likely driving a 5‑7% bump in subscription uptake.
  • Insurer Coverage Expansion: The recent agreement with the health‑system insurer will begin reimbursement in H2 2025 (ET) / 2025年下半年 (TPE). Covered members are projected to add $150 million of annual recurring revenue, boosting top‑line growth.
  • International Market Roll‑out: Dexcom plans to launch its CGM in Germany and Japan in early 2025 (ET) / 2025年初 (TPE). Those markets together represent a potential $300 million revenue opportunity within two years.
  • 新感測器上市：10 天佩戴感測器已獲 FDA 核准，預計於 2024 年第四季 (ET) / 2025 年第一季 (TPE) 上市。更長的使用壽命降低更換成本，預計可帶動訂閱用戶成長 5‑7%。
  • 保險覆蓋擴大：與健康保險系統的合作將於 2025 年下半年 (ET) / 2025 年下半年 (TPE) 開始給付。預估每年新增約 1.5 億美元的持續營收，提升營收增速。
  • 國際市場推廣：Dexcom 計畫於 2025 年初 (ET) / 2025 年初 (TPE) 在德國與日本上市。這兩大市場合計在兩年內可提供約 3 億美元的營收潛力。

RISKS

  • Regulatory Delay: If the FDA postpones clearance of the next‑gen sensor, subscription growth could stall, potentially shaving 3‑5% off annual revenue.
  • Reimbursement Pull‑back: A policy change that narrows insurer coverage could cut the projected $150 million revenue stream, causing a 4% earnings hit.
  • Competitive Pressure: Abbott’s FreeStyle Libre and emerging Chinese CGM makers are lowering prices; a 10% price erosion could compress Dexcom’s gross margin from 61.5% to the high 50% range.
  • 法規延遲：若 FDA 推遲次世代感測器核准，訂閱增長可能受阻，年度營收可能減少 3‑5%。
  • 給付縮減：若保險政策收緊，削減原先預估的 1.5 億美元收入，將使盈餘下降約 4%。
  • 競爭壓力：Abbott 的 FreeStyle Libre 及新興中國 CGM 廠商正削價競爭，若價格下跌 10%，Dexcom 毛利率可能從 61.5% 降至 50% 以上。

Buy at today’s $59.55 level. The stock trades at a forward P/E of 19.8, well below the 5‑year average of ~27, giving a clear valuation cushion while earnings are surging 92% YoY. Over the next five years the moat remains strong thanks to entrenched clinician habits, high switching costs, and premium pricing.
