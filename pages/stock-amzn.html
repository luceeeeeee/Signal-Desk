BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Amazon.com, Inc. runs the world’s largest online marketplace, delivering a huge variety of products directly to consumers and providing cloud‑computing services through Amazon Web Services (AWS). The company’s moat comes from three concrete sources: (1) a logistics network of fulfillment centers and last‑mile delivery that gives it a speed advantage none of the few rivals can match; (2) the Amazon Prime subscription which creates high switching costs because members receive free shipping, video, music and cloud perks in one bundle; (3) a dominant share of the U.S. e‑commerce market (about 40%) and a leading position in cloud‑infrastructure (over 30% of global market share).

業務概況

Amazon.com, Inc. 經營全球最大的線上商城，直接向消費者配送各類商品，同時透過 Amazon Web Services (AWS) 提供雲端運算服務。公司的護城河來自三個具體來源：(1) 由多座履約中心與最後一哩配送組成的物流網路，使其速度優勢難以被少數競爭者追上；(2) 透過 Amazon Prime 會員制，將免運費、影音、音樂與雲端福利打包，造成高切換成本；(3) 在美國電商市場佔約 40% 以及全球雲端基礎設施市場佔超過 30% 的領先地位。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Amazon announced that AWS revenue grew 12% year‑over‑year, pushing total company revenue to $213.4 billion in Q4 2025, a 13.6% increase versus the prior year. The firm also rolled out the “Prime Day” 2025 event, generating $12 billion in sales in a single weekend, and disclosed a $2 billion cost‑saving initiative focused on automation in fulfillment centers.

近期發展

過去六個月，Amazon 公布 AWS 營收年增 12%，帶動公司 2025 年第四季總營收達 2134 億美元，較去年同期成長 13.6%。公司亦推出 2025 年的「Prime Day」活動，單週末即創造 120 億美元銷售額，並披露一項 20 億美元的成本削減計畫，重點是履約中心的自動化。

CATALYSTS

  • AWS Innovation Launch: Introduction of next‑generation AI‑optimized instances in Q3 2025, expected to lift AWS revenue by 4‑6% annually.
  • Prime Membership Expansion: New bundled services (e.g., health, gaming) slated for Q4 2025, likely to increase Prime renewal rate by 1.5 percentage points, adding roughly $3 billion in incremental sales.
  • International Marketplace Growth: Aggressive push into Southeast Asia and India starting H2 2025, targeting a 15% increase in overseas GMV (gross merchandise volume) by 2026.

催化劑

  • AWS 創新發布：2025 年第三季推出新一代 AI 優化實例，預計每年提升 AWS 營收 4‑6%。
  • Prime 會員擴張：2025 年第四季新增健康、遊戲等綁定服務，預計將會員續訂率提升 1.5 個百分點，約增加 30 億美元的額外銷售。
  • 國際市場成長：自 2025 年下半年起在東南亞與印度加速布局，目標到 2026 年海外商品總額 (GMV) 增長 15%。

RISKS

  • Regulatory Crackdown: Antitrust investigations in the U.S. and EU could force divestitures of key logistics assets, potentially cutting operating cash flow by up to 5%.
  • Margin Pressure in Retail: Rising freight and labor costs may compress the retail gross margin from 50.3% to below 48% if inflation remains high through 2025.
  • AWS Competition: Accelerated growth of Microsoft Azure and Google Cloud could steal 2‑3% market share per year, slowing AWS revenue growth to single‑digit levels.

風險

  • 監管打擊：美國與歐盟的反托拉斯調查可能迫使公司出售關鍵物流資產，最高削減 5% 的營運現金流。
  • 零售毛利壓縮：若運費與勞工成本持續上升，零售部門毛利率可能由 50.3% 降至低於 48%，侵蝕利潤。
  • AWS 競爭：微軟 Azure 與 Google Cloud 的快速成長可能每年奪走 2‑3% 的市場份額，使 AWS 營收增速降至單位數。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s price of $265.06.
