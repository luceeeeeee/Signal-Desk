BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English:

Boeing designs, assembles and sells large commercial airplanes, defense aircraft, satellites and related services. It controls roughly 45% of the global market for wide‑body jets, and airlines face huge switching costs because pilots, maintenance crews and spare‑part inventories are all built around Boeing’s specific designs. The brand is synonymous with “big‑plane” capability, allowing Boeing to command a price premium over many rivals.

繁體中文：

波音設計、組裝並銷售大型商用飛機、軍用機、衛星及相關服務。它占全球寬體客機約 45% 的市場份額，航空公司要換供應商成本極高，因為飛行員、維修人員和備件庫存全部是圍繞波音機型設計的。波音品牌與「大型客機」同義，使其能在許多競爭者上取得價格溢價。
