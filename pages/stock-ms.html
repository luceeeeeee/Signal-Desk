BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Morgan Stanley is a global investment bank that helps companies raise money, trades stocks and bonds for clients, and manages wealth for individuals and institutions. Its moat comes from a deep client franchise built over more than a century, a brand that commands a premium, and a highly regulated “bank‑plus‑broker” model that makes it costly for new entrants to replicate the same mix of capital markets, wealth management, and research capabilities.

業務概況

摩根士丹利是一家全球性投資銀行，協助企業募資、為客戶執行股票與債券交易，並為個人與機構提供財富管理服務。其護城河來自超過百年的客戶關係網、品牌溢價以及「銀行加券商」的高度受監管模式，使新競爭者難以同時複製資本市場、財富管理與研究三大能力。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Morgan Stanley’s revenue jumped to $17.08 billion in Q3 2025, a 15% increase year‑over‑year, driven by record‑high trading volumes and strong wealth‑management inflows. The firm also lifted its profit margin, posting net income of $4.61 billion, the highest quarterly profit in the past three years.

最近動向

過去六個月，摩根士丹利的營收在 2025 年第三季升至 170.8 億美元，較去年同期成長 15%，主要受益於創紀錄的交易量與強勁的財富管理資金流入。公司亦提升獲利率，淨 profit 為 46.1 億美元，為過去三年最高的季報盈餘。

  • Cost‑Efficiency Program: A “lean‑bank” initiative aims to cut operating expenses by $500 million annually, improving operating margin by about 50 basis points starting FY2027.

  • 【財富管理擴張】：公司財富管理平台每年新增 2,500 億美元資產；若資產規模達到 1.5 兆美元，預計 12‑18 個月內盈餘可提升 8‑10%。

  • 【成本效率計畫】：「瘦身銀行」計畫目標每年削減 5 億美元營運支出，從 2027 財年起營業利潤率可提升約 50 個基點。
