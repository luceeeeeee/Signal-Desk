BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Analog Devices designs and sells high‑performance analog, mixed‑signal and digital signal‑processing chips that turn real‑world signals (temperature, pressure, sound, motion) into data for industrial equipment, automotive systems and consumer gadgets. The company holds roughly 30% market share in precision analog ICs and benefits from deep engineering expertise that makes its products hard‑to‑replace; customers often redesign entire hardware platforms to use ADI parts, creating high switching costs. Its strong relationships with tier‑1 OEMs and a portfolio of patented architectures give it a durable moat, though the semiconductor market remains capital‑intensive.

Analog Devices 設計並銷售高性能類比、混合訊號以及數位訊號處理晶片，將溫度、壓力、聲音、運動等實際訊號轉成資料，供工業設備、汽車系統與消費電子使用。公司在精密類比晶片約占 30% 市佔率，憑藉深厚的工程技術讓產品不易被取代；客戶常需重新設計整個硬體平台才能換用 ADI 產品，形成高切換成本。與一線 OEM 的緊密關係與多項專利架構提供了穩固的護城河，雖然半導體產業資本需求大。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months ADI posted a 30.4% year‑over‑year revenue surge, driven by booming demand for automotive radar and industrial IoT chips. The company also announced a $2 billion acquisition of a leading MEMS sensor maker, expected to close by Q4 2025 and add a new high‑margin product line.

在過去六個月，ADI 營收年增 30.4%，主要受惠於汽車雷達與工業物聯網晶片需求激增。公司亦宣布以 20 億美元收購一家領先的 MEMS 感測器廠商，預計於 2025 年第四季完成，將帶來新高毛利產品線。

  • Automotive Radar Roll‑out: ADI’s radar chips are now in three major EV models slated for production in Q3 2025; a 5% lift in automotive revenue could add $300 million to annual sales.
  • MEMS Acquisition Integration: Completion of the MEMS deal will broaden the addressable market to $15 billion and is projected to lift gross margin by 70 bps within 12‑18 months.
  • Industrial IoT Expansion: New long‑term contracts with two top‑tier industrial appliance makers, signed in early 2025, are expected to grow industrial revenue at 15% CAGR through 2028.
  • 汽車雷達推廣：ADI 的雷達晶片已被三款主要電動車型採用，預計於 2025 年第三季投產；若汽車收入提升 5%，全年銷售可增加 3 億美元。
  • MEMS 收購整合：收購完成後將把可服務市場擴大至 150 億美元，預計在 12‑18 個月內將毛利率提升 70 個基點。
  • 工業物聯網擴張：2025 年初與兩家頂級工業設備製造商簽署長期合約，預計至 2028 年工業收入以 15% 年複合增長率成長。
