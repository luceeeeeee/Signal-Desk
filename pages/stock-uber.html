BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Uber Technologies runs a global platform that connects riders with drivers, delivers food through Uber Eats, and offers freight and logistics services. It owns the largest ride‑hailing network in the United States (≈65% of rides) and a strong presence in over 70 countries, giving it scale that new entrants cannot quickly match. The moat comes from network effects – the more drivers on the platform, the more riders it attracts, and vice‑versa – plus a brand that consumers trust and a data‑rich algorithm that optimizes pricing and dispatch.

繁體中文

Uber 是全球性的线上平台，连接乘客与司机、提供 Uber Eats 食品外送，并涉足货运与物流服务。它在美国拥有约 65% 的乘车市场份额，并在 70 多个国家拥有强大布局，规模优势让新竞争者难以快速复制。护城河来源于网络效应——司机越多吸引更多乘客，乘客越多也吸引更多司机——以及被消费者信赖的品牌和可优化定价与派单的数據算法。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Uber’s quarterly revenue rose 20.1% YoY to $14.37 billion, driven by a 30% jump in Eats orders and a 15% increase in rides per active user. The company also turned cash‑flow positive in the most recent quarter, generating $2.1 billion of free cash flow, and announced a dividend‑free share‑repurchase program worth $2 billion.
