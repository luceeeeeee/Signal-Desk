BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Goldman Sachs is a global investment bank that earns money by advising corporations on mergers, underwriting stock and bond issues, and trading securities for its own account. Its moat comes from a combination of deep client relationships, a brand that commands a premium, and a regulatory “bank‑ing‑wall” that makes it hard for new entrants to replicate its full‑service platform. Roughly 40% of Fortune 500 CEOs say Goldman is their go‑to adviser, creating switching costs because firms rarely change advisers once trust is built.

企業概況

高盛是一家全球性投資銀行，主要賺取顧問費（協助企業併購）、承銷股票與債券以及自營證券交易的收入。它的護城河來自深厚的客戶關係、能夠取得溢價的品牌，以及規範上的“銀行牆”，讓新進者難以完全複製其全方位服務。約有 40% 的財富 500 強 CEO 表示高盛是他們首選顧問，因而形成高切換成本，企業不易更換顧問。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Goldman posted a 14.5% jump in revenue to $13.45 billion in Q4 2025 and a 24.3% surge in earnings, driven by record‑high trading volumes and strong demand for private‑placement financing. The firm also announced a $1 billion buyback program, signaling confidence in cash generation and returning capital to shareholders.

近期發展

過去六個月，高盛的收入在 2025 年第四季增至 134.5 億美元，年增率為 14.5%；盈餘則成長 24.3%，主要受交易量創新高與私人配售需求旺盛帶動。公司還宣布回購 10 億美元股票，顯示對現金產生能力的信心並將資金回饋股東。
