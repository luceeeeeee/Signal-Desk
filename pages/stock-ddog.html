BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Datadog provides cloud‑based monitoring software that helps companies watch the performance of their servers, applications and digital services in real time. Its platform aggregates millions of data points and uses AI to alert engineers before problems affect customers. The company controls roughly 15% of the global observability market, and customers face high switching costs because moving data pipelines and alert rules to a new vendor is technically complex and risky. This creates a modest moat, though large cloud providers could try to copy the service.
