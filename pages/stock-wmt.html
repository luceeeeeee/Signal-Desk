BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Walmart operates the world’s largest chain of discount stores, a grocery‑plus‑online platform, and a logistics network that ships millions of items daily. It controls roughly 25% of the U.S. grocery market and 15% of total retail sales, giving it unparalleled buying power with suppliers. The moat comes from three hard‑to‑replicate advantages: (1) a massive scale that forces low prices, (2) a trusted brand that draws price‑sensitive shoppers, and (3) a proprietary distribution system that limits competitors’ ability to match its same‑day delivery and in‑store pickup services.

繁體中文

Walmart 是全球最大的折價零售連鎖、兼具線上購物與物流網路，每天出貨數百萬件商品。它在美國雜貨市場約佔 25%，在全體零售銷售約佔 15%，因此擁有極強的議價能力。護城河主要有三點：① 巨大的規模迫使供應商降低價格，② 受信賴的品牌吸引對價格敏感的顧客，③ 專屬的配送系統讓競爭者難以匹配同日送達與到店取貨服務。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Walmart’s U.S. e‑commerce sales grew 12% year‑over‑year, while same‑store sales in its grocery division rose 3.4%, helping overall revenue climb 5.6% to $190.7 B in the January 2026 quarter. The company also announced a $12 B investment to expand its automated fulfillment centers, targeting a 15% boost in same‑day delivery capacity by 2027.

過去六個月，Walmart 美國線上銷售較去年同期成長 12%，雜貨同店銷售升 3.4%，推動 1 月 2026 季度營收提升 5.6% 至 1907 億美元。公司同時宣佈投入 120 億美元建設自動化履約中心，目標在 2027 年前提升 15% 的同日送達能力。

  • Automation Rollout: New robotic fulfillment hubs will cut labor costs and speed up delivery, potentially adding $3 B to annual earnings by FY2028.
  • Healthcare Services Expansion: Opening walk‑in clinics in 500 additional stores could generate $1 B in incremental revenue within three years.
  • International Market Re‑focus: Exiting low‑margin markets in Central America and reallocating capital to high‑growth Mexico could lift international comparable sales growth to 6% YoY by FY2029.

  • 【自動化推進】：新建機械化履約中心將降低人力成本、加速配送，預估至 2028 財年可為年度盈餘新增 30 億美元。
  • 【醫療服務擴張】：在 500 家門店增設即診診所，三年內可帶來 10 億美元額外營收。
  • 【國際市場再聚焦】：退出中美洲低利潤市場、把資金投入增長快速的墨西哥，預計至 2029 財年國際同店銷售年增率可達 6%。

RISKS

  • Consumer Spending Slump: A recession that cuts discretionary income could reduce same‑store sales by 2–3% each quarter, trimming annual profit by up to $4 B.
  • Supply‑Chain Disruption: A major port strike or cyber‑attack on the logistics network could delay deliveries, hurting the newly built same‑day delivery promise and eroding the competitive edge.
  • Regulatory Pressure on Pricing: New antitrust legislation targeting “price‑fixing” practices might force Walmart to raise prices, weakening its low‑price advantage and market share.

Buy at today’s $131.93 level because the company’s scale, brand and logistics moat still earn it a 25% share of U.S. grocery sales and a clear path to earnings upside from automation and health services.
