BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Huntington Ingalls Industries (HII) builds and services large U.S. warships – aircraft carriers, submarines, and destroyers – for the Navy and Coast Guard. It also runs a ship‑repair yard, provides modernization upgrades, and offers logistics support for the fleet. The moat is strong: the U.S. Department of Defense awards multi‑year contracts that lock in billions of dollars, and switching to a new builder would cost the government years of re‑qualification, massive engineering redesigns, and loss of existing support infrastructure. In short, the “who builds our warships” market is effectively closed to new entrants, giving HII a durable competitive advantage.

營運概況

亨廷頓英戈爾斯產業 (HII) 為美國海軍與海岸警衛隊建造及維修大型軍艦，包括航空母艦、潛艦與驅逐艦，並經營船舶維修廠、提供升級改造與後勤支援。 其護城河相當堅固：美國國防部簽訂多年期合約鎖定數十億美元，換成其他廠商需要多年重新認證、巨額工程重新設計以及失去現有支援基礎設施，成本極高。 簡言之，「誰來造軍艦」的市場基本上不向新進者開放，給予 HII 持久的競爭優勢。

---

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the first half of 2024 HII landed three new shipbuilding contracts worth $4.5 billion, pushing quarterly revenue growth to 18% year‑over‑year. At the same time, the company announced a $200 million cost‑saving program that is expected to lift gross margin by roughly 1 point in 2025.

最近動向

2024 上半年，HII 獲得三項總額 45 億美元的新造艦合約，使得季營收年增率提升至 18%。 同時，公司公佈 2 億美元的節支計畫，預期將在 2025 年將毛利率提升約 1 個百分點。

---

CATALYSTS

  • New Navy Contract Awards – The Navy’s FY25 “Surface Combatant” and “Submarine” solicitation are expected to be awarded by Q4 2024, potentially adding $6‑$8 billion of construction backlog and driving revenue growth above 20% YoY.
  • Ship Modernization Program Expansion – A recently announced $1.2 billion mid‑life upgrade program for existing fleet vessels will start in HII’s shipyard in early 2025, boosting earnings margin.
  • Strategic Partnership with L3Harris – A joint venture to integrate advanced radar systems onto new destroyers is slated for commercial launch in mid‑2025, creating a new high‑margin revenue stream.

催化劑

  • 新海軍合約授予 – 海軍 2025 財政年度的「水面戰鬥艦」與「潛艦」招標預計於 2024 年第四季落標，可能為公司帶來 60‑80 億美元的建造訂單，營收年增率可突破 20%。
  • 艦艇升級計畫擴大 – 最近公佈的 12 億美元現役艦艇中期升級計畫將於 2025 年初在 HII 船廠啟動，提升獲利率。
  • 與 L3Harris 的戰略合作 – 雙方將在新驅逐艦上整合先進雷達系統，預計於 2025 年中正式商用，帶來高毛利的新收入來源。

---

RISKS

  • Defense Budget Cuts – A prolonged downgrade in U.S. defense spending could shrink the pipeline of new ship contracts, potentially cutting future revenue by 10‑15% and dragging earnings lower.
  • Program Delays & Cost Overruns – Complex shipbuilding projects often run late or over budget; a single major delay could erode the margin lift from the cost‑saving program and hurt cash flow.
  • High Debt Load – With $3 billion of debt and only $0.8 billion of cash, any rise in interest rates or tightening of credit markets could increase financing costs and limit flexibility for additional acquisitions.

風險

  • 國防預算削減 – 若美國防務開支持續下修，新船合約管線將減少，未來營收可能下滑 10‑15%，盈餘受壓。
  • 專案延遲與成本超支 – 大型造船專案常有延遲或超支的風險，單一重大延遲即可抵銷節支計畫帶來的毛利提升，影響現金流。
  • 高負債 – 公司負債 30 億美元，現金僅 8 億美元，若利率上升或信貸環境緊縮，融資成本將上升，且收購彈性受限。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s $364 level. The forward P/E of 17.9 is below the 5‑year average of about 20, and analysts target a price around $420, implying ~15% upside. A breach of $390 would unlock the next tranche of Navy contracts and substantiate a move to “Buy.” Over the next five years the shipbuilding moat remains very strong because the U.S. government’s shipyard selection process is costly and politicized, making HII’s market position durable.

結論
