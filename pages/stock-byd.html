BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English: Boyd Gaming runs a network of casinos and hotels mostly in the United States, focusing on mid‑scale resort properties in Nevada, Mississippi, Arkansas and other regional markets. The company’s moat comes from its high‑cost licenses to operate gaming facilities, longstanding relationships with state regulators, and a loyalty program that locks in repeat gamblers who earn points across all Boyd locations. Because gaming licenses are scarce and the brand is trusted by both tourists and local patrons, a well‑funded newcomer would struggle to acquire the required licenses and replicate the same customer stickiness.
