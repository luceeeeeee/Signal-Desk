BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Stellantis N.V. (ticker STLA) designs, builds and sells cars and light trucks under 14 global brands such as Jeep, Ram, Peugeot and Fiat. The company’s scale gives it bargaining power with suppliers and the ability to spread R&D costs across many models, which is a modest moat. However, the auto industry is highly competitive and low‑cost rivals (especially from Asia) can undercut prices, so the moat is narrow and relies mainly on brand loyalty and a broad dealer network.

業務概況

Stellantis N.V.（代號 STLA）設計、製造並銷售包括 Jeep、Ram、Peugeot、Fiat 在內的 14 個全球品牌的汽車與輕型卡車。公司的規模讓它在供應商議價上有優勢，且研發成本可在多個車型間分攤，形成一定的護城河。然而，汽車產業競爭激烈，尤其是亞洲低成本競爭者可以壓低價格，故護城河較窄，主要依賴品牌忠誠度與廣泛的經銷商網路。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Stellantis reported a 6.5% year‑over‑year revenue increase, driven by strong U.S. Jeep and Ram sales and a modest recovery in European markets. The company also announced a $2 billion cost‑saving program aimed at improving its thin 5.6% gross margin.

最近動向

過去六個月，Stellantis 的營收年增率為 6.5%，主要受益於美國 Jeep 與 Ram 的強勢銷售以及歐洲市場的緩慢復甦。公司同時宣布一項 20 億美元的成本削減計畫，目標提升僅 5.6% 的毛利率。

CATALYSTS

  • New EV Platform Rollout: Launch of a low‑cost electric vehicle architecture in 2025 could boost margins by targeting price‑sensitive buyers; initial volume expected in Q3 2025.
  • U.S. Tax Credit Expansion: Proposed extension of the federal EV tax credit to $7,500 through 2026 would make Stellantis’s EV models more affordable, potentially adding $1.5 billion in U.S. sales.
  • Strategic Alliance with Battery Supplier: A long‑term supply deal signed in March secures battery capacity at a fixed price, reducing input cost risk and supporting the EV rollout.

催化劑

  • 【新電動車平台推出】：2025 年推出低成本電動車平台，可提升毛利率，首批量產預計於 2025 年第三季。
  • 【美國稅收抵免擴大】：建議將聯邦電動車稅抵免提升至 7,500 美元並延長至 2026 年，將使 Stellantis 的電動車更具吸引力，或為美國帶來 15 億美元銷售增長。
  • 【與電池供應商的戰略聯盟】：3 月簽署長期供電協議，鎖定電池價格，降低原料成本風險，支撐電動車推廣。

RISKS

  • Supply‑Chain Bottleneck: A renewed semiconductor shortage could halt production, cutting quarterly revenue by up to 8% and pressuring earnings.
  • Weak European Demand: If inflation remains high in the Eurozone, car purchases may stall, lowering the expected 5% growth in Europe and dragging overall revenue.
  • Currency Headwinds: A stronger euro versus the dollar would increase the cost of imported components, eroding the already thin gross margin.

風險

  • 【供應鏈瓶頸】：若半導體短缺再次出現，可能導致產能停擺，季度營收減少最高 8%，盈餘受壓。
  • 【歐洲需求疲弱】：若歐元區通脹持續走高，車輛購買意願可能下降，預期歐洲 5% 成長將受影響，拖累整體營收。
  • 【匯率逆風】：歐元兌美元升值將提高進口零件成本，進一步侵蝕原本已很薄的毛利率。

CONCLUSION

At a forward P/E of 4.0, Stellantis trades at a deep discount to its 5‑year average (≈7×) and offers a clear upside if the EV platform and U.S. tax credit materialize. I recommend a Buy at the current $7.28 level, with a target of $9.50 within 12 months; a break below $6.50 would force a reassessment. The company’s moat is narrow but likely to hold for the next five years provided it delivers on cost cuts and EV scale.

結論

以 4.0 倍的預期本益比來看，Stellantis 相對過去五年平均約 7 倍的估值有大幅折扣，若電動車平台與美國稅收抵免順利落實，將有明顯上行空間。我給予買進建議，現價 $7.28，目標價 12 個月內達 $9.50；若跌破 $6.50，則重新評估。公司的護城河較窄，但只要成功實現成本削減與電動車規模，未來五年仍具持續性。
