BUSINESS OVERVIEW / 企業概覽

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) runs Google, the world’s dominant search engine that handles about 92% of global web searches. It also owns YouTube, the leading video platform, and sells advertising through its ad network, which captures the lion’s share of digital ad spend in the United States and Europe. The moat is strong: search users rarely switch because of habit and data‑driven relevance, and advertisers stay because Google’s algorithms deliver the best return on their spend. In addition, the brand’s scale lets it negotiate better prices for data‑center power and hardware, keeping costs low.

Alphabet Inc.（GOOGL）經營 Google，全球搜尋引擎市場佔有率約 92%，同時擁有 YouTube 這一全球最大影片平台，並透過其廣告網絡銷售廣告，佔美國與歐洲數位廣告支出的絕大多數。護城河非常堅固：搜尋使用者因習慣與精准度不易更換，廣告主則因 Google 演算法提供最佳投資回報而留下。再者，品牌規模讓公司在資料中心電力與硬體採購上取得更低成本，維持低費用。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS / 最近發展

In the last six months Google’s ad revenue grew 18% year‑over‑year, pushing total revenue to $113.8 billion in Q4 2025. The company also announced a $5 billion investment in AI chips for its data centers, aiming to cut processing costs by up to 15% within two years.

過去六個月，Google 的廣告收入較去年同期增長 18%，使 2025 年第四季總營收達 1,138.3 億美元。公司同時宣佈投入 50億美元 用於資料中心的 AI 晶片，目標在兩年內將運算成本降低最多 15%。

CATALYSTS / 促成因素

  • AI‑Driven Advertising Boost: New generative‑AI tools will let advertisers auto‑create campaigns, likely lifting ad spend by 5‑8% in fiscal 2026. Expect impact starting Q2 2026.
  • Cloud Services Expansion: Google Cloud aims to add 200 new enterprise contracts this year, which could add $3 billion to annual revenue if each contract averages $15 million.
  • Regulatory Relief: Ongoing antitrust cases in the U.S. and EU are slated for decision by mid‑2026; a favorable outcome could remove a $10 billion shadow‑cost on future ad pricing.
  • AI廣告提振：新一代生成式 AI 工具讓廣告主自動產生廣告素材，預計 2026 財年廣告支出將提升 5‑8%。影響將於 2026 年第二季開始顯現。
  • 雲端服務擴張：Google Cloud 目標今年再簽 200 個企業合約，若每筆平均 1500萬美元，將為年度營收額外貢獻 30億美元。
  • 監管寬減：美國與歐盟的反托拉斯案件預計於 2026 年中作出裁決；若結果有利，將減少約 100億美元的廣告定價陰影成本。

RISKS / 風險

  • Ad‑Revenue Concentration: A 10% slowdown in U.S. digital ad spend (triggered by an economic recession) would cut annual earnings by roughly $15 billion, dragging the stock down 12‑15%.
  • AI Competition: If rivals like Microsoft/OpenAI capture a larger share of enterprise AI services, Google Cloud could miss its $30 billion revenue target, compressing margins by 200 basis points.
  • Regulatory Penalties: A worst‑case antitrust fine of $5 billion, coupled with forced data‑sharing mandates, would increase operating costs by about 1% of revenue, reducing net margin from 31% to 28%.
  • 廣告收入集中：若美國數位廣告支出因經濟衰退下滑 10%，將使年度盈餘減少約 150億美元，股價可能下跌 12‑15%。
  • AI 競爭：若微軟/OpenAI 在企業 AI 服務上奪取更大市場份額，Google Cloud 可能無法達到 300億美元收入目標，毛利率將被壓縮 200 個基點。

Buy at today’s price of $384.80. The stock trades at a forward P/E of 28.4, roughly in line with its 5‑year average (≈27), and analysts target a 12% upside, giving a reasonable entry point.
