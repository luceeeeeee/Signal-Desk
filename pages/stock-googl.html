BUSINESS OVERVIEW / 業務概況

Alphabet Inc. runs Google, the world’s dominant internet search engine, and a suite of related services such as YouTube, Gmail and the Android operating system. Google processes more than 8 billion searches per day, giving it control of roughly 92 % of the global search market. The moat comes from its massive data advantage—more searches feed better AI, which makes the search experience harder for any newcomer to copy—and from the Android ecosystem, which locks billions of devices into Google’s app and advertising platform.

Alphabet Inc. 旗下的 Google 是全球最大的網路搜尋服務，還擁有 YouTube、Gmail 以及 Android 作業系統等相關產品。Google 每天處理逾 80 億次搜尋，佔全球搜尋市場約 92 %。護城河在於龐大資料優勢——搜尋量越多，AI 越精準，讓新進者難以匹敵；以及 Android 生態系，使數十億裝置必須使用 Google 的應用與廣告平台。
