BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Alphabet Inc. (GOOGL) runs the world’s largest online search engine, digital‑advertising platform, and a suite of popular services like YouTube, Android, and Google Cloud. Its moat comes from three concrete sources: (1) a dominant 92% share of global search traffic that creates a data feedback loop, (2) a massive network of advertisers who lock in budgets because switching to another platform would lose years of audience data, and (3) a brand that commands a premium – advertisers pay roughly 40% more on Google than on most rivals for comparable reach.

业务概览

Alphabet Inc.（GOOGL）經營全球最大線上搜尋引擎、數位廣告平台，以及 YouTube、Android、Google Cloud 等一系列熱門服務。其護城河來源有三點： (1) 全球搜尋流量 92% 的主導份額形成資料回饋循環， (2) 大量廣告主鎖定預算，換平台會失去多年累積的受眾資料， (3) 品牌溢價——廣告主在 Google 上的投放費用大約比大多數競爭對手高出 40%。
