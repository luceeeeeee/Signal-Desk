BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Palantir Technologies builds data‑integration software that lets large organisations combine, clean and analyse huge amounts of information in a single platform. Its flagship product, Foundry, is used by government agencies, defence contractors and big‑tech firms, while Apollo powers the deployment of software at cloud‑scale. The moat comes from deep integration into mission‑critical workflows and massive switching costs – once a client’s analysts have built custom data pipelines inside Palantir, moving to a rival would require rebuilding years of logic and re‑training staff. Palantir also benefits from a handful of long‑term government contracts that lock in revenue for several years.

Palantir Technologies 建立資料整合軟體，讓大型組織能在同一平台上彙整、清理、分析巨量資訊。其核心產品 Foundry 為政府機構、國防承包商與大型科技公司所使用，Apollo 則負責在雲端規模部署軟體。護城河來自於深度嵌入關鍵作業流程與極高的切換成本——客戶的分析師在 Palantir 內部建置的客製化資料流程若要搬遷至競爭者，必須重新打造多年邏輯並重新培訓人員。Palantir 亦擁有少數長期政府合約，確保多年收入。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Palantir posted FY 2025 revenue of $4.37 billion, a 70% year‑over‑year increase, driven by strong uptake of Foundry in the commercial sector. The company announced a new “AI‑Accelerated” module that adds generative‑AI capabilities to its platform, and secured an additional $300 million multi‑year contract with the U.S. Department of Defense.

在過去六個月，Palantir 公布 2025 財年營收 43.7 億美元，較去年同期增長 70%，主要受惠於企業部門 Foundry 的強勁採用。公司推出「AI 加速」模組，為平台加入生成式 AI 能力，並與美國國防部簽下額外 3 億美元的多年合約。
