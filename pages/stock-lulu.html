BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Lululemon designs, manufactures and sells high‑end athletic apparel, primarily yoga and sports‑wear, through its own stores and website. The brand commands a roughly 40% price premium over average gym‑wear competitors, and its loyal customer base rarely switches because product fit and community‑driven classes create strong switching costs. Lululemon controls about 12% of the premium athleisure market in North America and is expanding rapidly in Asia, giving it a durable moat built on brand strength and an integrated “fit‑feedback” ecosystem.

Lululemon 主要設計、製造與販售高價位的運動服飾，尤其是瑜珈與運動穿搭，通路包括自營門市與官網。 旗下品牌可取得約 40% 的價格溢價，且顧客因為產品合身度與社群課程形成高切換成本，很少換品牌。 其在北美高端運動服市場約佔 12%，同時在亞洲快速擴張，憑藉品牌力與「合身回饋」生態系形成持久護城河。

Despite the revenue lift, net income slowed to $590 million as marketing spend surged 15% to fund the new digital initiative.
