BUSINESS OVERVIEW

AbbVie is a global drug maker that sells prescription medicines, most notably the blockbuster heart‑failure drug irbesartan and the immunology product Humira. Its moat comes from a portfolio of patented biologics that require complex manufacturing and decades‑long clinical data, giving it strong switching costs—doctors and patients stay on a drug once it works because changing can be risky and costly. Humira alone still holds about 40% of the worldwide psoriasis market, letting AbbVie charge a premium price. Outside of its patented drugs, the company has little brand‑based advantage, so the moat narrows once patents expire.

繁體中文

雅培是全球藥品製造商，主要銷售處方藥，最著名的產品包括心衰藥 irbesartan 和免疫藥 Humira。 其護城河來自一系列需複雜製程和多年臨床資料的專利生物製劑，形成高換藥成本——醫師與患者一旦使用有效藥物，轉換的風險與成本都很高。 Humira 仍佔全球牛皮癬市場約 40%，讓雅培能以溢價定價。 除了專利藥外，品牌優勢較弱，專利失效後護城河將會縮小。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months AbbVie reported 12.4% revenue growth, driven by strong sales of its newly approved oncology drug Riabni and continued Humira demand in emerging markets. However, earnings fell 45.8% because of a $2.5 B charge for a failed Phase III trial in rheumatoid arthritis and higher tax expenses.

過去六個月，雅培的營收增長 12.4%，主要受新獲批的腫瘤藥 Riabni 與新興市場 Humira 需求提升的推動。 但盈餘下降 45.8%，原因是因類風濕性關節炎第三期試驗失敗產生 25億美元 的一次性費用，以及稅項支出上升。

  • Cost‑Saving Initiative: A announced $1 B cost‑reduction program targeting manufacturing efficiencies is slated to begin in FY2026, improving operating margin by roughly 3 percentage points.

  • Humira 生物仿製藥上市：雅培自有的生物仿製藥 Amjevita 預計於 2026 下半年在美國上市，可抵消 Humira 銷售下滑，維持現金流。
