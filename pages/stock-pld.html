BUSINESS OVERVIEW / 企業概況

English

Prologis owns and operates a global network of logistics warehouses that landlords and e‑commerce firms rent to store and ship goods. It controls roughly 10% of the worldwide industrial real‑estate market, giving it scale to negotiate long‑term leases at premium rates. The moat comes from location advantage – most of its sites sit within a 5‑mile radius of major ports, highways or population centers – and from high switching costs: tenants sign multi‑year contracts and moving a distribution center costs millions of dollars, which locks customers in.

繁體中文

Prologis（普洛吉斯）擁有並營運全球物流倉庫，提供給房東與電商公司存放與出貨商品。它大約掌握全球工業不動產市場 10% 的份額，規模足以爭取到高於市場的長期租金。護城河在於地點優勢——大多數資產都位於離主要港口、主要高速公路或人口中心 5 英里內——以及高換成本：租客簽多年合約，搬遷配送中心的成本高達數百萬美元，讓客戶難以離開。

  • E‑commerce demand tailwind: Global online sales are projected to grow 12% YoY through 2028, driving higher space utilisation and allowing Prologis to raise rents. Impact expected in 2024‑2026 with incremental EBITDA of $0.8‑$1.2 billion.
