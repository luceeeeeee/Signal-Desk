BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English

Salesforce (CRM) builds cloud‑based software that lets companies manage customer relationships, sales pipelines, marketing campaigns and service requests—all through a web browser. It dominates the Customer Relationship Management (CRM) market with roughly 35% share, making it the clear platform of choice for large enterprises. The moat comes from three hard‑to‑copy strengths: a massive installed base that creates network effects, high switching costs because data and workflows are deeply embedded, and a brand that commands a premium price over smaller rivals.
