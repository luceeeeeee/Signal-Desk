BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Exxon Mobil extracts crude oil and natural gas, refines them into gasoline, diesel and chemicals, and sells the finished fuels worldwide. It also owns a large network of retail gas stations and a chemicals business that turns oil into plastics and other products. The company’s moat comes from three hard‑to‑copy strengths: (1) control of roughly 8% of global upstream oil production, giving it access to low‑cost reserves; (2) a globally‑integrated supply chain that lets it move barrels from field to pump‑station without paying third‑party fees; and (3) a brand that is trusted by millions of drivers, allowing it to keep pricing power at the pump.

企業概況

Exxon Mobil 開採原油與天然氣，將之煉製成汽油、柴油與化學品，並在全球販售成品油。它同時擁有龐大的零售加油站網以及將石油轉為塑膠等產品的化學事業。公司的護城河有三大難以複製的優勢：（1）掌握約 8% 的全球上游石油產量，取得低成本油源；（2）全流程整合的供應鏈，讓原油從油田到加油站皆自行運作，省下第三方費用；（3）深受全球消費者信賴的品牌，讓它在油價上有一定的溢價空間。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Exxon reported a 4% drop in quarterly revenue as oil prices fell, but the company cut capital spending by $7 billion, strengthening its balance sheet. It also announced a $2 billion share‑repurchase program and raised its dividend by 10% to $1.25 per share, signaling confidence in cash flow.

最近發展

過去六個月內，由於油價下跌，Exxon 的季營收下降了 4%，但公司減少 70 億美元資本支出，讓財務狀況更穩健。公司同時宣布回購 20 億美元股票，並將每股股息提高 10% 至 1.25 美元，顯示對現金流的信心。

CATALYSTS

  • Higher Oil Prices: A sustained rise in Brent crude to $90 +/ €85 per barrel would lift upstream earnings sharply; expectation is for price recovery to begin in Q4 2024 (Oct – Dec).
  • Chemical Margin Expansion: New‑type low‑carbon plastics plants coming online in 2025 could boost chemicals gross margin by 2‑3 percentage points, adding $1‑$1.5 billion annual profit.
  • Strategic Acquisitions: A possible purchase of a niche LNG regasification asset in 2025 would diversify cash flow and could add $500 million of EBITDA within two years.

催化劑

  • 油價上升：布倫特原油若穩定在每桶 90 美元（或每公升 85 歐元）以上，將大幅提升上游盈餘；預期 2024 年第四季（10‑12 月）開始回升。
  • 化學事業毛利改善：2025 年新建的低碳塑膠廠將使化學品毛利提升 2‑3 個百分點，年增利潤約 10‑15 億美元。
  • 策略性收購：2025 年可能收購一項小型液化天然氣再氣化資產，可於兩年內貢獻約 5 億美元的 EBITDA，提升現金流多元性。

---

RISKS

  • Oil Price Collapse: A sudden drop in Brent below $70 / €65 would cut upstream profit by more than $15 billion, dragging earnings down 20% YoY.
  • Debt Burden: With $50.5 billion of debt and only $10.7 billion of cash, any sustained decline in cash flow could force asset sales at a discount, eroding shareholder value.
  • Regulatory Crackdown: New U.S. carbon‑pricing or stricter ESG rules could increase operating costs by $2‑$3 billion annually, pressuring net margins.

風險

  • 油價大跌：若布倫特跌破每桶 70 美元（或每公升 65 歐元），上游利潤將縮減逾 150 億美元，年度盈餘同比下降超過 20%。
  • 負債壓力：公司負債 505 億美元，現金僅 107 億美元，若現金流長期疲弱，可能被迫低價出讓資產，侵蝕股東價值。
  • 監管收緊：美國若實施碳稅或更嚴格的 ESG 規範，將使營運成本增加 20‑30 億美元，壓縮淨利率。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s $154.33 price only if you believe oil will rebound above $85 per barrel by early 2025 and the chemicals expansion will deliver the expected margin boost. If Brent stays under $75 for more than two quarters, cut the position. The company’s integrated model gives it a strong moat for the next five years, but the high debt load and regulatory risk keep upside modest.

結論

僅在您相信油價在 2025 年初能回升至每桶 85 美元以上，且化學事業的毛利提升能如預期發揮時，才在目前 154.33 美元的股價買入。若油價連續兩個季度跌破 75 美元，則應減碼。公司的全產業鏈模式在未來五年仍具強大護城河，但高負債與監管風險限制了上行空間。
