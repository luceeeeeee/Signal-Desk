BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Exxon Mobil explores, extracts, refines, transports and sells oil and natural‑gas worldwide. It runs more than 3,000 km of pipelines and owns a global network of refineries that lets it turn crude into gasoline, diesel and chemicals at a lower cost than most rivals. Its moat comes from massive real‑asset scale, long‑term contracts that lock in demand, and a brand that lets it price products about 5‑10% higher than unknown independents. New entrants would need billions of dollars and decades of permitting to match this footprint, which makes competition very hard.

企業概況

Exxon Mobil 在全球進行油氣的勘探、開採、精煉、運輸與銷售。它擁有超過 3,000 公里的管線以及遍布全球的煉油廠，能以低於大多數競爭者的成本把原油轉換成汽油、柴油與化學品。其護城河來自龐大的實體規模、鎖定需求的長期合約，以及能讓產品比不知名獨立廠高出 5‑10% 的品牌優勢。新進者必須投入數十億美元並花上數十年才能取得類似的佈局，競爭壁壘相當高。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Exxon posted a 6% drop in quarterly revenue to $80 billion and earnings fell 11% year‑over‑year, largely because oil prices slipped below $80 per barrel. The company announced a $5 billion share‑repurchase program and a $2 billion increase in its capital‑expenditure budget for new low‑carbon projects, signaling a shift toward cleaner‑energy assets.

最近發展

過去六個月 Exxon 的季營收下降 6% 至 800 億美元，盈餘同比下降 11%，主要因油價跌破每桶 80 美元。公司宣佈執行 50 億美元的回購計畫，並將低碳項目資本支出提升 20 億美元，顯示其正轉向更清潔的能源資產。

CATALYSTS

  • Oil‑price rebound: A sustained rise in Brent crude to $90‑$95 per barrel would lift upstream earnings by roughly 15% YoY. Timing: next 3‑6 months if OPEC+ tightens supply.
  • Low‑carbon project ramp‑up: Completion of the Port Arthur CCUS hub and the Baytown hydrogen plant could add $1‑$2 billion of cash flow annually. Timing: 2025‑2026.
  • Share‑repurchase acceleration: If the board expands the buyback to $10 billion, earnings per share would rise and support the stock price. Timing: announced 2024 Q3, likely executed in 2024‑2025.

催化劑

  • 【油價回升】：若布蘭特油價穩定在 90‑95 美元/桶，將使上游部門盈餘年增約 15%。時機：若 OPEC+ 收緊供給，未來 3‑6 個月內可能發生。
  • 【低碳項目提速】：Port Arthur 二氧化碳捕集與貯存設施以及 Baytown 氫能工廠完工後，年現金流可增加 10‑20 億美元。時機：2025‑2026 年。
  • 【回購加速】：若董事會將回購規模擴大至 100 億美元，將提升每股盈餘並支撐股價。時機：2024 年第三季已宣布，預計 2024‑2025 年執行。

RISKS

  • Oil‑price slump: A prolonged dip below $70 per barrel would cut upstream profit by more than 20%, dragging overall earnings down sharply.
  • Regulatory crackdown: New U.S. climate rules that limit methane leaks or impose carbon taxes could raise operating costs by $3‑$5 billion annually.
  • Capital allocation misstep: Over‑investing in low‑carbon projects that delay cash‑flow generation may erode free cash flow and force dividend cuts.

風險

  • 【油價暴跌】：若油價長期低於每桶 70 美元，上游利潤會縮減超過 20%，將大幅拖累整體盈餘。
  • 【監管收緊】：美國新頒布的減碳法規（如甲烷洩漏限制或碳稅）可能每年增加 30‑50 億美元的營運成本。
  • 【資本配置失誤】：若在低碳項目上過度投入而延遲現金流回收，將侵蝕自由現金流，甚至迫使公司削減股息。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s $154.33 level because the forward P/E of 15.2 is well below the 5‑year historical average of about 20, giving a clear margin of safety. The stock is attractive as long as oil stays above $80 per barrel and the low‑carbon projects begin to generate cash in 2025. If Brent slides under $70 or the dividend is cut, the thesis collapses. The moat—massive asset base and brand premium—should remain strong for the next five years, provided the company manages the energy transition wisely.
