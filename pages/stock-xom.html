BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Exxon Mobil is a global oil‑and‑gas company that explores for crude, refines it into fuels, and sells gasoline, diesel, petrochemicals and power‑generation services worldwide. It owns about 12% of global upstream oil production and runs the world’s largest refining capacity, giving it tight control over supply and pricing. The moat comes from massive capital assets, long‑term contracts with governments, and a brand that commands a premium in retail fuel markets; no new entrant can cheaply replicate this scale.

企業概覽

Exxon Mobil 是一家全球性的油氣公司，從地下開採原油、將其煉製成汽油、柴油、石化產品，並在全球銷售燃料與電力服務。它擁有約 12% 的全球上游產量，並經營全球最大規模的煉油設施，讓它能掌握供應與定價。護城河來自於龐大的資本資產、與各國政府的長期合約，以及在零售燃料市場上能取得溢價的品牌；新進者無法以低成本快速複製這種規模。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Exxon cut its upstream capital spending by $5 billion, citing lower oil prices and a strategic shift toward lower‑carbon projects. Q3‑25 revenue fell 0.5% YoY to $83.3 billion, while net income dropped 7% to $7.55 billion, reflecting weaker margins. The company announced a $10 billion share‑buyback program to support the stock price.

最近動向

過去六個月，Exxon 因油價走低與轉向低碳項目，將上游資本支出削減 50 億美元。2025 年第三季營收同比下降 0.5% 至 833.3 億美元，淨利下降 7% 至 75.5 億美元，顯示毛利率受壓。公司同時宣佈執行 100 億美元的回購計畫，以支撐股價。

  • Lower‑Carbon Portfolio Expansion: Investment in hydrogen, carbon‑capture and renewable power is expected to generate $5 billion of incremental earnings by 2029, with the first commercial hydrogen plant slated for 2026.
  • OPEC+ Production Cuts: Anticipated supply reductions from OPEC+ in Q4‑25 could lift crude prices 8‑12%, directly boosting Exxon’s upstream profit margin.
  • Share‑Buyback Execution: If the $10 billion buyback is completed by mid‑2026, earnings per share could rise by ~4%, supporting a higher stock price.

催化劑

  • 【低碳投資擴張】：投資氫氣、碳捕集與再生能源，預計到 2029 年帶來 50 億美元的額外盈餘，首座商業氫氣廠預計 2026 年投產。
  • 【OPEC+ 減產】：預估 2025 年第四季 OPEC+ 供應縮減將推高原油價格 8%~12%，直接提升上游毛利。
  • 【回購執行】：若在 2026 年中前完成 100 億美元回購， 每股盈餘可提升約 4%，有助股價上揚。

RISKS

  • Oil‑Price Collapse: A prolonged drop in Brent crude below $70 per barrel, triggered by an economic slowdown, would cut upstream earnings by up to 20%, dragging net income below $5 billion.
  • Regulatory Carbon Costs: New U.S. carbon‑tax legislation could add $2 billion in annual compliance costs, compressing margins.
  • Project Execution Delays: If the 2026 hydrogen plant is delayed beyond 2028, the expected earnings boost is postponed, weakening the growth narrative.
