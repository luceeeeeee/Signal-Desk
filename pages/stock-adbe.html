BUSINESS OVERVIEW / 企業概況

Adobe makes software that lets people create and edit digital media – things like photos, videos, PDFs and websites. Its flagship products (Photoshop, Illustrator, Acrobat and the cloud‑based Creative Cloud suite) are used by 90%+ of professional designers, advertisers and publishers, giving Adobe a dominant share of the creative‑software market. The moat is strong: customers pay yearly subscriptions and switching costs are high because assets, fonts and workflows are stored in Adobe’s cloud, and the brand commands a 30‑40% price premium over cheaper alternatives.

Adobe 主要提供讓人製作與編輯數位內容的軟體，包括照片、影片、PDF 與網站等。其核心產品（Photoshop、Illustrator、Acrobat 以及雲端版 Creative Cloud）被超過 90% 的專業設計師、廣告公司與出版業者使用，幾乎壟斷了創意軟體市場。護城河極為堅固：客戶採用年度訂閱，若要換平台必須搬移大量素材、字體與工作流程，且 Adobe 品牌可取得比廉價替代品高 30‑40% 的價格溢價。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS / 最近動向

In the last six months Adobe’s FY2025 Q4 revenue jumped 12% year‑over‑year to $6.40 billion, driven by continued growth in Creative Cloud subscriptions and expanding demand for its Document Cloud services. The company also announced a 15% price increase for new Creative Cloud plans starting July, which is expected to lift forward earnings by roughly $0.30 per share.

過去六個月，Adobe 2025 財年第四季營收年增 12% 至 64 億美元，主要受益於 Creative Cloud 訂閱持續成長以及 Document Cloud 需求提升。公司亦宣佈 7 月起新 Creative Cloud 計畫將上調 15% 價格，預計將使未來每股盈餘增加約 0.30 美元。

CATALYSTS / 催化劑

  • Creative Cloud Expansion: New AI‑powered features (Adobe Firefly) are rolling out across Photoshop and Illustrator this quarter, likely boosting subscription renewal rates by 3‑4% and adding $0.15‑$0.20 EPS.
  • Document Cloud Monetisation: A revised pricing tier for Adobe Sign and PDF services takes effect in Q3, projected to generate an extra $250 million of annual revenue.
  • Enterprise Deal Wins: Adobe secured a multi‑year contract with a Fortune 500 retailer to integrate its Experience Cloud, expected to close $400 million in new recurring revenue by FY2026.
  • Creative Cloud 擴張：全新 AI 功能（Adobe Firefly）本季在 Photoshop 與 Illustrator 推出，預計可提升訂閱續約率 3‑4%，貢獻每股盈餘 0.15‑0.20 美元。
  • Document Cloud 營收成長：Adobe Sign 與 PDF 服務的新定價層級於第三季生效，預估每年新增營收 2.5 億美元。
  • 企業大單：Adobe 獲得一家《財富 500 強》零售商的多年合約，將其 Experience Cloud 整合入平台，預計到 2026 財年帶來 4 億美元的經常性收入。

RISKS / 風險

  • Subscription Churn Surge: If economic slowdown forces customers to cut discretionary spending, renewal rates could dip 5%, shaving roughly $0.35 off forward EPS.
  • Regulatory Scrutiny: Ongoing antitrust investigations into Adobe’s market dominance in creative software could lead to fines or forced divestitures, impacting cash flow.
  • AI Competition: Rapid advances from rivals (e.g., Canva, Microsoft Designer) could erode Adobe’s pricing power, especially if they offer comparable AI tools at lower subscription fees.
  • 訂閱流失上升：若經濟衰退導致客戶削減非必要支出，續約率可能下降 5%，將使未來每股盈餘減少約 0.35 美元。
  • 法規審查：針對 Adobe 在創意軟體市場的主導地位之反托拉斯調查持續進行，若遭罰款或被迫剝離業務，將衝擊現金流。
  • AI 競爭：如 Canva、Microsoft Designer 等競爭對手快速推出同等 AI 功能且價格較低，可能削弱 Adobe 的定價能力。

Buy at today’s $246.10 level.
