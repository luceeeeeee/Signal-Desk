BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English: Crown Castle (CCI) owns, operates and leases a nationwide network of cell‑tower sites and small‑cell infrastructure that mobile operators rent to run their 5G and 4G networks. It controls roughly 20% of the U.S. tower market, giving it pricing power and the ability to bundle sites for carriers. The moat is strong: towers are capital‑intensive, require local permits, and carriers face high switching costs because moving antennas would disrupt service and cost millions.

繁體中文：Crown Castle（CCI）擁有、營運並出租全美範圍的手機基地台與小型基地設施，行動通訊業者租用這些資產來建置 4G、5G 網路。它約掌握全美 20% 的基地台市場，因而有定價權並能為電信公司提供打包方案。護城河相當堅固：建造基地台需要龐大資本與地方建築許可，且電信業者若要搬遷天線會中斷服務且花費上千萬美元，換句話說切換成本極高。

The company announced a $2 billion buy‑back plan and began installing 5G small cells in 30 new metros, aiming to capture higher‑margin urban space.
