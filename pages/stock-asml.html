BUSINESS OVERVIEW

ASML makes the ultra‑precise machines that chip makers use to print the tiny circuits on silicon wafers. Its flagship product, extreme ultraviolet (EUV) lithography, is the only one capable of producing the sub‑5‑nanometer nodes that the industry's leading firms need. ASML controls about 70 % of the global EUV market, and switching to a different supplier would require chipmakers to re‑tool multi‑billion‑dollar fabs, creating extremely high switching costs. This lock‑in, combined with patents that protect the EUV technology, gives ASML a strong moat that few well‑funded rivals can breach.

商業概況

ASML 製造半導體製程中用來在矽晶圓上印製電路的超高精密機台。其最重要的產品是極紫外光 (EUV) 光刻機，唯一能製造 5 奈米以下先進製程的設備。ASML 佔全球 EUV 市場約 70%，若晶圓廠要換供應商，需投入上十億美元的設備改造，形成極高的轉換成本。再加上保護 EUV 技術的專利，使 ASML 擁有 堅實的護城河，難以被資金充裕的競爭者撼動。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months ASML posted 13.2% revenue growth, driven by record EUV sales to leading foundries such as TSMC and Samsung. The company also announced a $1.5 billion price increase for its next‑generation EUV tools, which is expected to boost margins further.

最近動向

過去六個月，ASML 的 營收成長 13.2%，主要來自向台積電、三星等頂尖代工廠的 EUV 訂單創新高。公司同時宣佈 15億美元 的下一代 EUV 設備價格上調，預期將進一步提升毛利率。

CATALYSTS

  • New EUV Shipments Q4 2024: Delivery of the next‑gen NXE:3400 to TSMC and Samsung will expand capacity and could lift revenue by 5‑7% in FY2025.
  • Advanced Node Adoption: If major customers move 3‑nm and 2‑nm production to volume in 2025, ASML’s EUV tool demand could surge, adding $1‑1.5 billion to annual sales.
  • Regulatory Approval for EUV‑Plus: Expected clearance from the EU in H2 2025 will allow a higher‑power EUV system, potentially unlocking a new price tier and widening the margin gap.

催化劑

  • 【第四季新 EUV 出貨】：向台積電、三星交付下一代 NXE:3400，將擴大產能，預計 2025 財年營收提升 5‑7%。
  • 【先進製程採用】：若主要客戶在 2025 年大規模投產 3 nm、2 nm 產品，EUV 設備需求可能激增，為年度營收再加 10‑15 億美元。
  • 【EUV‑Plus 法規核准】：預計 2025 年下半年取得歐盟許可，將推出更高功率 EUV 系統，開創新價格層級並擴大毛利差距。

RISKS

  • Supply‑Chain Disruption: A prolonged shortage of high‑purity lasers or critical optics could delay EUV shipments, cutting FY2025 revenue by up to $2 billion.
  • Geopolitical Restriction: If the U.S. or EU imposes new export controls on EUV technology to China, ASML could lose a growing portion of its sales pipeline, reducing growth to single‑digit levels.
  • Customer Capital Constraints: Should major fabs like TSMC face cash‑flow pressure and defer EUV purchases, ASML’s margin expansion from price hikes would be muted.

風險

  • 【供應鏈中斷】：高純度激光或關鍵光學元件短缺可能導致 EUV 交付延遲，2025 財年營收最多縮減 20 億美元。
  • 【地緣政治限制】：若美國或歐盟對 EUV 技術出口中國實施新管制，ASML 可能失去增長迅速的市場，成長率降至個位數。
  • 【客戶資金壓力】：若台積電等大客戶資金緊張而推遲 EUV 訂單，ASML 透過提價提升的毛利空間將受限。
