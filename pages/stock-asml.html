BUSINESS OVERVIEW

ASML designs and manufactures the extreme‑ultraviolet (EUV) lithography machines that are essential for producing the most advanced semiconductors. Over 90% of the world’s leading chipmakers use ASML’s EUV tools, and the machines cost more than $150 million each, creating a huge barrier for any new entrant. The moat comes from unrivaled technology patents, a decades‑long supply chain partnership with chip fabs, and the fact that once a fab installs an EUV line it faces years of costly re‑qualification to switch to a rival.

业务概览

ASML 設計與製造極紫外光 (EUV) 光刻機，這是製造最先進半導體不可或缺的設備。全球超過 90% 的領先晶圓廠使用 ASML 的 EUV 機台，且每台售價超過 1.5 億美元，形成極高的進入壁壘。護城河來自無可匹敵的技術專利、與晶圓廠長達數十年的供應鏈合作，及一旦晶圓廠安裝 EUV 生產線後，轉換至競爭對手將需多年且成本高昂。

---

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months ASML shipped a record 16 EUV systems, pushing Q1 2026 revenue up 13.2% year‑over‑year to $8.77 billion. The company also announced a 20% increase in the EUV high‑NA (numerical aperture) roadmap, promising a 2‑3× jump in chip density once the next generation machines ship in 2027‑2028.

最近發展

過去六個月，ASML 交付了創紀錄的 16 台 EUV 系統，使 2026 年第一季營收比去年同期增長 13.2%，達到 87.7 億美元。公司同時公布 EUV 高數值孔徑（high‑NA）路線圖將提升 20%，預計 2027‑2028 年新一代機台上市後，晶片密度將提升 2‑3 倍。

---

CATALYSTS

  • Next‑Gen High‑NA EUV Launch: The first high‑NA EUV tool is scheduled for customer acceptance in Q4 2026; it could unlock 30%‑40% higher lithography productivity, driving a fresh wave of orders.
  • China’s Chip Re‑equipment Cycle: If Chinese fabs accelerate their EUV adoption in 2027, ASML could capture an additional $2 billion in annual revenue.
  • Supply‑Chain Tightening: Ongoing shortages of laser‑source components may force customers to pre‑pay for future capacity, boosting ASML’s cash flow and reducing revenue volatility.

催化劑

  • 下一代高數值孔徑 (High‑NA) EUV 啟動：首台高 NA EUV 機預計於 2026 年第四季交付客戶驗收；此機可提升 30%‑40% 的光刻產能，將帶動新一波訂單。
  • 中國晶圓再裝備週期：若中國晶圓廠在 2027 年加速採用 EUV，ASML 有望額外創造 20 億美元年營收。
  • 供應鏈緊縮：激光光源零件持續短缺可能迫使客戶提前預付未來產能，提升 ASML 現金流並降低營收波動。

---

RISKS

  • Geopolitical Export Restrictions: New U.S. or EU rules limiting EUV sales to China could cut projected 2027 revenue by up to $3 billion, as China is a fast‑growing demand source.
  • Technology Disruption: If an alternative lithography method (e.g., direct‑write nano‑imprint) achieves commercial viability, ASML could lose market share; the downside would be a 15%‑20% earnings decline.
  • Customer Concentration: The top three customers (TSMC, Samsung, Intel) account for roughly 55% of revenue; a slowdown at any of them would immediately hit earnings, potentially dropping the stock 10%‑12% in a quarter.

風險

  • 地緣政治出口限制：若美國或歐盟出台新規限制 EUV 向中國出口，2027 年的營收預估可能減少至多 30 億美元，因中國是快速成長的需求來源。
  • 技術顛覆：若其他光刻技術（如直接寫入納米印刷）取得商業化，可望奪走 ASML 市場份額，預計導致 15%‑20% 營收下滑。
  • 客戶集中度：前三大客戶（台積電、三星、英特爾）佔營收約 55%，其中任一客戶放緩都會立刻影響盈餘，股價在一季內可能下跌 10%‑12%。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s price of $1,438.99. The forward P/E of 30.1 is below the 5‑year average of 34, giving a modest discount, and the pipeline of high‑NA EUV machines promises a new revenue surge. A sustained drop below $1,200 would force a reassessment to hold. Over the next five years the EUV moat looks durable because the technology and ecosystem lock‑in are unlikely to be broken by new entrants.

結論
