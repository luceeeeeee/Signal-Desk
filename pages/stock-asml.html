BUSINESS OVERVIEW / 業務概況

ASML designs and sells the huge, highly precise machines that chip factories need to print the tiniest patterns onto silicon wafers. Its flagship product, extreme‑ultraviolet (EUV) lithography tools, is the only technology capable of making chips at 5 nm and smaller nodes. ASML controls roughly 70 % of the global EUV market, and customers face massive switching costs because a fab must redesign its whole production line to use a different vendor’s equipment. The company’s patents, long‑term customer relationships, and the sheer engineering difficulty of EUV give it a strong moat that no new entrant can easily breach.

ASML 設計並銷售晶圓廠必須使用的巨大且極其精密的機台，用來在矽晶圓上印製最細微的電路圖樣。其主打產品極紫外光 (EUV) 光刻機是唯一能製造 5 nm 及以下晶片的技術。ASML 佔全球 EUV 市場約 70 % 的份額，客戶若改用其他供應商必須全面重新規劃產線，換供應商的成本極高。公司擁有大量專利、長期客戶關係以及 EUV 的高工程門檻，構成了新競爭者難以突破的強大護城河。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS / 最近發展

In the last six months ASML posted Q1 2026 revenue of $8.77 billion, up 13 % year‑over‑year, and its EUV sales grew 18 % as major foundries accelerated next‑generation node rollouts. The company also announced a $1 billion share‑buyback program, signaling confidence in cash flow and further supporting the stock price.

過去六個月，ASML 公佈 2026 年第一季營收 87.7 億美元，較去年同期成長 13 %，且 EUV 機台銷售增長 18 %，因主要晶圓廠加速推動下一代製程。公司同時宣布 10 億美元的回購計畫，顯示對現金流的信心並進一步支撐股價。

  • EUV Expansion in China: Chinese fabs plan to install three new EUV lines in 2027, creating roughly $2 billion of incremental sales for ASML.
  • New High‑NA EUV Launch: The next‑generation high‑numerical‑aperture EUV system, scheduled for customer shipments in Q4 2026, promises a 20 % price premium and unlocks sub‑3 nm chips, expanding the addressable market.
  • Supply‑Chain Stabilization: Recent easing of semiconductor component shortages reduces lead times for EUV parts, allowing ASML to increase annual delivery capacity by 10 % in 2026.
  • 中國 EUV 擴張：中國晶圓廠計畫在 2027 年安裝三條新 EUV 生產線，為 ASML 帶來約 20 億美元的新增銷售。
  • 新一代高 NA EUV 推出：下一代高數值孔徑 (High‑NA) EUV 機台預計於 2026 年第四季開始交付，能以約 20 % 的價格溢價賣出，並解鎖 3 nm 以下製程，擴大可服務市場。
  • 供應鏈穩定：近期半導體零件短缺緩解，使 EUV 零件交期縮短，ASML 2026 年年交付量可提升 10 %。

RISKS / 風險

  • Geopolitical Export Restrictions: If the U.S. or EU imposes tighter export curbs on EUV technology to China, ASML could lose up to $3 billion in projected 2027 sales, pressuring earnings.
  • EUV Yield Challenges: Persistent technical issues that reduce chip yield on new EUV nodes could slow customer spend, cutting ASML’s order backlog by 15 % in the next two quarters.
  • Macroeconomic Slowdown: A global recession that cuts semiconductor capital spending by 10 % would trim ASML’s revenue growth, potentially dropping the forward P/E to 20 and the stock price by 20 %.
  • 地緣政治出口限制：若美國或歐盟對中國的 EUV 技術實施更嚴格的出口禁令，ASML 可能失去至2027 年約 30 億美元的預期銷售，侵蝕盈利。
  • EUV 良率問題：若新一代 EUV 製程的良率持續低於預期，客戶支出會放緩，導致未來兩季訂單積壓縮減 15 %。
  • 宏觀經濟衰退：全球經濟衰退使半導體資本支出下降 10 %，將把 ASML 的收入成長拉低，預估前瞻本益比跌至 20，股價可能跌 20 %。
