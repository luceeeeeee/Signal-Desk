BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Blackstone is a global alternative‑asset manager that raises money from investors and puts it into private‑equity buyouts, real‑estate, credit and hedge‑fund strategies. Its moat comes from a massive, long‑standing network of limited‑partner relationships and a reputation that lets it win the most competitive deals, often at a premium price that others cannot match. The firm also benefits from scale‑economies: once a fund is closed, the costs of sourcing and managing assets are spread over billions of dollars, making it hard for a new entrant to replicate the same fee income.

Blackstone announced a $4.5 billion increase in committed capital for its flagship private‑equity fund, pushing total commitments above $90 billion. The firm also completed the acquisition of a leading data‑center operator, adding a stable, inflation‑protected cash flow stream.
