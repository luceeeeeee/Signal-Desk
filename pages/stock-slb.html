BUSINESS OVERVIEW

SLB (formerly Schlumberger) provides tools, technology, and services that help oil‑and‑gas producers find and extract hydrocarbons from underground reservoirs. Its core strengths are a worldwide network of field service teams, a vast proprietary data library, and patented drilling‑automation software that few rivals can match. The company controls roughly 35% of the global oil‑field services market, and its customers face high switching costs because changing service providers means re‑training crews, re‑qualifying equipment, and risking costly downtime.

企業概況

SLB（前稱史倫貝爾）提供油氣公司用來勘探與開採地下油氣的工具、技術與服務。它的核心優勢是遍佈全球的現場服務團隊、龐大的自有資料庫，以及少數競爭者能比的專利鑽井自動化軟體。公司約佔全球油田服務市場的 35%，客戶換供應商需重新訓練人員、重新認證設備，且會冒停工風險，因而產生高切換成本。

---

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months SLB secured three major offshore contracts worth an estimated $1.2 billion, boosting quarterly revenue by 4% QoQ. At the same time, the company announced a $400 million cost‑reduction program focused on digitizing field operations, which is expected to lift gross margin by roughly 150 basis points by FY2027.

近期發展

過去六個月，SLB 獲得三項總價約 12 億美元的離岸大型合約，使季營收環比增長 4%。同時，公司宣佈了一項 4 億美元的成本削減計畫，重點在於將現場作業數位化，預計到 2027 財年毛利率可改善約 150 個基點。

---

CATALYSTS

  • Offshore Expansion: New contracts in the Gulf of Mexico and West Africa will drive top‑line growth of $600 million–$800 million annually, ramping up over the next 12–18 months.
  • Digital Automation Rollout: Deployment of the company's proprietary drilling‑automation platform is projected to cut field‑service labor costs by 8%, adding roughly $200 million to EBITDA by FY2026.
  • Energy‑Transition Services: Growing demand for carbon‑capture and enhanced‑oil‑recovery projects could add $300 million of incremental revenue per year starting in 2025.

催化劑

  • 【離岸擴張】：在墨西哥灣與西非的新合約每年將貢獻 6–8 億美元營收，未來 12–18 個月內逐步推進。
  • 【數位自動化推廣】：公司專屬的鑽井自動化平台預計能將現場服務人力成本降低 8%，到 2026 財年為止將為 EBITDA 增加約 2 億美元。
  • 【能源轉型服務】：碳捕集與增產油回收的需求增長，預計自 2025 年起每年可帶來 3 億美元的額外營收。

---

RISKS

  • Oil‑Price Decline: A sustained drop in crude prices below $70 /bbl could delay customers’ capital spending, cutting SLB’s revenue by up to 5% and compressing margins.
  • Execution of Cost Cuts: If the $400 million digitization program fails to meet its timeline, the anticipated margin uplift may not materialize, leaving earnings below consensus.
  • Regulatory Headwinds: Stricter environmental regulations on offshore drilling could reduce new contract pipelines, impacting growth in the company’s highest‑margin segments.

風險

  • 【油價下跌】：若原油價格長期低於每桶 70 美元，客戶資本支出會受抑制，營收可能下降 5% 以內，毛利率受壓。
  • 【成本削減執行】：若 4 億美元的數位化計畫未能按時完成，預期的毛利率提升將無法實現，盈利可能低於市場共識。
  • 【法規阻礙】：更嚴格的離岸鑽探環保法規可能削減新合約的來源，衝擊公司最高毛利率的業務板塊。

CONCLUSION

At $56.88, SLB is a Buy. The forward P/E of 17× is well below its 5‑year average of ~22×, indicating the stock is undervalued relative to its earnings power. The recent offshore wins and a credible cost‑cutting roadmap should lift margins and earnings, outweighing the modest earnings decline this year. A breach below $52 would force a reassessment, while a sustained rally above $62 would signal the market has fully priced in the upside. The moat—global scale, proprietary data, and high switching costs—appears robust for the next five years.
