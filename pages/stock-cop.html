BUSINESS OVERVIEW / 事業概覽

English

ConocoPhillips is a pure‑play oil and gas explorer‑producer that finds, drills and sells crude oil and natural gas worldwide. It holds long‑term leasehold interests in about 12% of U.S. onshore production acreage, giving it a sizable share of the most cost‑efficient basins. The moat comes from its low‑cost asset base and a portfolio of high‑margin fields where switching to a competitor would require new drilling permits, huge capital spend and years of regulatory approval.

繁體中文

ConocoPhillips 是一家純粹的油氣勘探與生產公司，負責在全球尋找、鑽探並銷售原油與天然氣。它在美國陸上產區約佔 12% 的長期租賃權，掌握了成本最具效率的油氣盆地。護城河在於其低成本的資產組合及高毛利率的油氣田，若要換成競爭者則必須重新取得鑽探許可、投入巨額資本並等待多年審批，門檻相當高。

  • Oil price volatility: A prolonged drop in Brent below $70 /barrel would cut quarterly earnings by more than 15%, eroding cash flow and forcing further capex cuts.
