BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Mastercard runs a global network that lets merchants accept credit‑, debit‑, and prepaid cards, and it processes the electronic payments behind those cards. It commands roughly 25% of worldwide card network volume, and retailers stay on Mastercard because switching to another network would require re‑programming terminals, renegotiating contracts, and losing the consumer trust attached to the Mastercard brand. The network effect – more merchants accept the card, more consumers use it – creates a high barrier for new entrants, even those with deep pockets.

繁體中文

Mastercard 经营一个全球网络，让商家可以接受信用卡、借记卡和预付卡，并处理这些卡背后的电子支付。它大约掌握全球卡网络交易量的 25%，商家之所以继续使用 Mastercard，是因为更换网络需要重新编程终端、重新谈判合约，并且会失去消费者对 Mastercard 品牌的信任。网络效应——使用该卡的商家越多，消费者使用的意愿越高——为即使是资本雄厚的新竞争者也设下了高门槛。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Mastercard reported revenue growth of 15.8% year‑over‑year, driven by a 13% rise in transaction volume and higher fee pricing on cross‑border payments. The company also launched a new AI‑powered fraud‑prevention suite that it says will cut charge‑back costs for merchants by up to 20% and increase its data‑monetization revenue stream.

繁體中文

在過去六個月，Mastercard 的營收年增率達 15.8%，主要來自交易量增長 13% 以及跨境支付手續費的提價。公司同時推出了一套 AI 驅動的防詐欺系統，稱可為商家減少最高 20% 的退單成本，並提升資料變現的收入。

CATALYSTS

  • Cross‑border fee hike: Mastercard plans to raise international transaction fees by 5‑7% starting Q3 2026, which should lift annual net revenue by roughly $2‑3 billion.
  • AI fraud‑prevention rollout: Full commercial adoption of its AI suite is expected by Q4 2025, unlocking a new $500 million recurring revenue line.
  • U.S. consumer spending rebound: A projected 4% increase in U.S. disposable income through 2026 will boost card usage volumes, adding an estimated $1 billion to top‑line growth.

  • 【跨境手續費上調】：Mastercard 計畫於 2026 年第三季將國際交易費用提升 5‑7%，預計將年度淨收入提升約 20‑30 億美元。
  • 【AI 防詐欺系統全面推廣】：AI 防詐欺套件預計於 2025 年第四季全面商業化，可開啟 5 億美元的經常性收入來源。
  • 【美國消費支出回升】：預估 2026 年美國可支配收入將增長 4%，將提升卡片使用量，為營收再增加約 10 億美元。

RISKS

  • Regulatory fee caps: If the U.S. or EU imposes stricter limits on interchange fees, Mastercard’s pricing power could be squeezed, potentially cutting forward earnings by 5‑8%.
  • Cyber‑attack disruption: A successful large‑scale breach of the network could damage merchant confidence and force costly security upgrades, hurting both revenue and margin.
  • Economic slowdown: A prolonged recession would reduce consumer card spending, lowering transaction volume and trimming net income by an estimated $1‑2 billion per year.
