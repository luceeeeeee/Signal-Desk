BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Lam Research makes the machines that chip makers use to carve tiny patterns into silicon wafers. Its equipment is essential for producing the most advanced memory (like DRAM) and logic chips, and the company controls roughly 30% of the global wafer‑fab equipment market in those segments. The moat comes from high switching costs – factories redesign their whole production line when they change vendors – and a deep patent portfolio that protects its process technologies. Because every new generation of chips requires a custom tool, well‑funded rivals find it costly and time‑consuming to win business from Lam.

業務概覽

Lam Research 生產半導體製造設備，幫助晶圓廠在矽片上刻出極細微的電路圖樣。其設備在先進記憶體與邏輯晶片領域佔全球大約 30% 的市場份額。護城河在於 高轉換成本——換供應商需要重新設計整條產線，還有 龐大的專利組合 保護其製程技術。新一代晶片的推出必須使用客製化設備，使資金雄厚的競爭者難以快速奪取 Lam 的客戶。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Lam posted 23.8% revenue growth year‑over‑year, driven by strong demand for 3‑nanometer and advanced memory tools. The company announced a $1.5 billion capital‑expenditure plan to expand its R&D capacity, aiming to stay ahead of the upcoming 2‑nanometer node. Earnings per share rose 41% compared with the same quarter a year ago, beating consensus estimates.

最近發展

過去六個月，Lam 的營收 年增 23.8%，主要受 3 奈米與先進記憶體設備需求刺激。公司宣佈 15 億美元的資本支出計畫，用於擴充研發產能，以領先即將到來的 2 奈米製程。每股盈餘較去年同期 成長 41%，超過市場預期。

CATALYSTS

  • 2‑Nanometer Ramp‑Up: Beginning Q4 2026 (ET) / 2026年10月 (TPE), Lam expects its new 2 nm etch tools to enter qualifying production. Successful qualification could add $800 M–$1 B of revenue annually.
  • Memory Supply Boom: Global DRAM and NAND inventories are projected to tighten through 2027, pushing fab spending on memory equipment up 12% YoY. Lam’s dominant position in memory tools should translate into faster order growth.
  • Strategic Partnerships: A recent joint‑development agreement with a leading foundry to co‑design next‑gen patterning technology could lock in multi‑year volume and expand Lam’s addressable market.

催化劑

  • 2 奈米量產升溫：預計於 2026 年第四季 (ET) / 2026年10月 (TPE) 進入客戶驗證階段。若驗證成功，年度營收可額外增加 8億至10億美元。
  • 記憶體供應緊張：全球 DRAM 與 NAND 庫存預計在 2027 年前持續收緊，推動記憶體設備支出年增 12%。Lam 在記憶體設備的領導地位將帶來更快的訂單增長。
  • 策略夥伴關係：與一家領先代工廠的共同開發協議，旨在設計下一代圖案技術，能鎖定多年量產，同時擴大 Lam 的可服務市場。

---

RISKS

  • Supply‑Chain Bottleneck: Any shortage of critical components (e.g., specialty gases, advanced optics) could delay tool deliveries and shave 5‑10% off quarterly revenue.
  • Customer Concentration: The top three foundries represent ≈40% of Lam’s sales; a slowdown at any of them (e.g., due to macro slowdown in consumer electronics) would materially depress earnings.
  • Technological Disruption: A breakthrough in alternative lithography or plasma‑free etching could render Lam’s current toolset less relevant, potentially shrinking market share by 10%+ over the next 3‑5 years.

風險

  • 供應鏈瓶頸：若關鍵元件（如特種氣體、高階光學元件）出現短缺，可能導致設備交付延遲，使季營收下降 5‑10%。
  • 客戶集中度：前三大代工廠佔 Lam 銷售約 40%，若其中任何一家因宏觀經濟放緩（例如消費電子需求減弱）而減產，將嚴重拖累盈餘。
  • 技術突變：若出現取代傳統等離子蝕刻的全新光刻或非等離子蝕刻技術，Lam 現有設備可能變得不具競爭力，未來 3‑5 年內市場份額可能下降 10% 以上。

CONCLUSION

Buy at the current $257.86 price. The forward P/E of 32.7 is well below the 5‑year average of ~40, giving a clear valuation upside, and the company’s strong moat and growth pipeline justify a ~15% near‑term target. A miss on the 2‑nanometer ramp‑up or a drop below $230 would make me reassess. Over the next five years the moat looks durable thanks to high switching costs and a deep IP base.
