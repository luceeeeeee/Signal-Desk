BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Apple designs, manufactures, and sells consumer electronics (iPhone, iPad, Mac, Apple Watch), services (App Store, iCloud, Apple Music) and software.

Its moat is strong: the iPhone holds roughly 20% of the global smartphone market, but more importantly the ecosystem creates high switching costs because apps, media, and data are tied to a single Apple ID. The brand allows Apple to charge about a 40% price premium versus Android rivals, and the services business adds sticky recurring revenue.

業務概況

Apple 設計、製造並銷售消費性電子產品（iPhone、iPad、Mac、Apple Watch）、服務（App Store、iCloud、Apple Music）以及軟體。

護城河非常強大：iPhone 佔全球智慧手機約 20% 市佔，但更關鍵的是生態系統造成高切換成本，因為應用程式、媒體與資料都綁在單一 Apple ID。品牌讓 Apple 能比 Android 競爭對手收取約 40% 的價格溢價，服務業務則提供持續的黏性收入。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Apple launched the iPhone 15 Pro series, which drove a 16.6% year‑over‑year revenue increase to $143.8 billion in Q4 2025. Services revenue grew 12% YoY, pushing overall gross margin to 48.2% in the same quarter.

最近動向

過去六個月 Apple 推出 iPhone 15 Pro 系列，使 Q4 2025 營收同比增長 16.6%至 1,437.6 億美元。服務收入同比增長 12%，使同季毛利率提升至 48.2%。

CATALYSTS

  • New product rollout (iPhone 16 / AR headset): A refreshed iPhone line and the first Apple AR headset could add another 5‑7% revenue lift, likely in fiscal Q2 2026.
  • Services expansion (AI‑driven apps, Apple Pay growth): Growing AI features and international Apple Pay adoption could boost services revenue by 10% annually, adding margin because services cost less to run.
  • Supply‑chain cost improvements: Securing cheaper component pricing and higher volume of in‑house chip production (M3) may lift gross margin by 0.5‑1.0 percentage points after Q3 2025.

催化劑

  • 【新產品發布（iPhone 16 / AR 頭戴裝置）】：全新 iPhone 系列與首款 AR 頭戴裝置預計在 2026 財年第2季推出，可再為營收帶來 5‑7% 的增長。
  • 【服務業務擴張（AI 應用、Apple Pay 成長）】：AI 功能與國際 Apple Pay 的擴大有望使服務收入年增 10%，而服務的成本較低，將提升整體獲利。
  • 【供應鏈成本改善】：透過更便宜的零件與自製 M3 晶片的大量產出，毛利率有望在 2025 財年第3季後提升 0.5‑1.0 個百分點。

RISKS

  • Supply shortage or chip delay: If TSMC or other fabs cannot deliver enough M3 chips, iPhone production could slip, shaving 3‑5% off quarterly revenue.
  • Regulatory pressure on App Store: Antitrust rulings that force Apple to allow alternative payment systems could cut services margin by up to 2 percentage points.
  • Consumer demand slowdown: A broader macro slowdown that reduces discretionary spending could cause iPhone sales to fall 4% YoY, pulling overall earnings down by $5 billion.

風險

  • 【供應短缺或晶片延遲】：若台積電或其他代工無法交付足夠的 M3 晶片，iPhone 產量受限，季度營收可能下滑 3‑5%。
  • 【App Store 監管壓力】：反托拉斯裁決若迫使 Apple 接受其他支付系統，服務業務的毛利率可能降低至 2 個百分點。
  • 【消費需求放緩】：宏觀經濟疲軟導致非必需支出下降，iPhone 銷售或全年下降 4%，使整體盈餘減少約 50 億美元。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s $271.35 level. The stock trades at a forward P/E of 28.9, well below its 5‑year historical average of ~33, giving a clear margin of safety. The combination of a durable ecosystem moat and accelerating services growth justifies a near‑term upside target of $300. A break below $250 or a sustained decline in services margin would force a reassessment. The moat looks solid for the next five years because switching costs and brand premium are unlikely to erode quickly.

結論

在目前 271.35 美元的價格下建議買入。此股的預估本益比 28.9 明顯低於過去五年平均約 33，提供明顯的安全邊際。堅固的生態系統護城河加上快速增長的服務業務，使股價有望升至 300 美元。若股價跌破 250 美元或服務毛利持續下滑，則需重新評估。未來五年護城河仍然堅固，因為切換成本與品牌溢價不易快速削弱。
