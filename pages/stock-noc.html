BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Northrop Grumman designs, builds and maintains advanced aircraft, space systems, missile defense and cyber‑security solutions for the U.S. government and allied militaries. Its moat comes from deep integration into the Department of Defense’s long‑term procurement contracts, a portfolio of patented technologies, and the high switching cost of moving a multi‑billion‑dollar defense program to a new supplier – the Pentagon rarely switches vendors once a platform is in production. Roughly 80% of its revenue comes from the U.S. defense budget, giving the company a stable, government‑backed customer base.

北羅普·格魯曼（Northrop Grumman）設計、製造並維護先進的飛機、太空系統、導彈防禦與網路安全解決方案，主要服務美國政府與同盟國軍隊。其護城河在於與美國國防部的長期採購合約深度捆綁、擁有多項專利技術，以及將數十億美元防務計畫轉移至新供應商的高切換成本——國防部一旦平台進入量產階段，基本不會換供應商。約80%的營收來自美國國防預算，為公司提供穩定且受到政府支持的客戶基礎。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Northrop Grumman secured a $2 billion contract extension for its B‑21 bomber program, pushing total program backlog above $25 billion. Quarterly earnings showed an 84.9% jump in net earnings YoY, driven by higher defense spending and a 4.4% rise in revenue despite a modest 20.5% gross margin.

過去六個月，北羅普·格魯曼獲得了20億美元的B‑21轟炸機計畫合約延期，使整體訂單積壓超過250億美元。季報顯示淨利潤同比大升84.9%，主要受國防開支上升與營收成長4.4%帶動，毛利率仍維持在20.5%左右。

CATALYSTS

  • B‑21 Production Ramp‑Up: Full‑scale low‑rate production is slated for fiscal 2026 (July 2026 ET / 2026年7月台北時間). Each aircraft adds roughly $400 million of annual revenue, potentially lifting FY‑27 revenue by 3‑4%.
  • Space Systems Growth: The launch of a new satellite‑bus platform for the U.S. Space Force is expected to begin deliveries in FY‑2025, adding an estimated $1 billion in backlog and a 5% boost to revenue over the next two years.
  • Cyber‑Security Contract Win: A recently announced $500 million multi‑year contract for cyber‑defense services could lift operating cash flow by $150 million annually once fully staffed.
  • 【B‑21量產提升】：《B‑21轟炸機》低速量產預計於2026財年（2026年7月美東時間）啟動，每架飛機約貢獻4億美元年營收，可能讓2027財年營收提升3‑4%。
  • 【太空系統擴張】：新衛星平台為美國太空部隊服務，預計2025財年開始交付，將新增約10億美元的訂單積壓，未來兩年營收可提升約5%。
  • 【網路安全合約】：最近簽下5億美元的多年度網路防禦服務合約，全面執行後每年可提升營運現金流1.5億美元。
