BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Meta Platforms operates the world’s largest social networking apps—Facebook, Instagram, WhatsApp—and a growing family of virtual‑reality products under the Meta brand.

The company’s moat comes from three concrete sources: 1) a network effect where billions of users keep each other on the platforms, making it hard for newcomers to attract a critical mass; 2) deep data‑driven ad‑targeting that yields a 20% higher return‑on‑ad‑spend for advertisers, locking in budgets; 3) a brand that commands a premium, with advertisers willing to pay more for Meta’s reach than for lesser platforms.

商業概況

Meta Platforms（前身 Facebook）營運全球最大的社交媒體平台，包括 Facebook、Instagram、WhatsApp，以及日益擴大的 Meta VR 產品。

公司的護城河有三個具體來源：1）網路效應——數十億使用者相互黏著，使新進者難以達到臨界規模；2）深度資料驅動的廣告精準投放，讓廣告主的廣告回報率比其他平台高約 20%，因此廣告預算被鎖定；3）品牌溢價，廣告主願意為 Meta 的觸及付出更高價格，形成價格壁壘。
