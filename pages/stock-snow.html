BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Snowflake provides a cloud‑based data‑warehousing platform that lets companies store, query and share massive amounts of data instantly, without building their own hardware. Its moat comes from a network effect: as more customers load data, the platform’s marketplace of data‑sets and integrations becomes richer, making it harder for a newcomer to offer the same breadth. Switching costs are high because moving petabytes of data and rewriting analytics pipelines is time‑consuming and risky. Snowflake also enjoys a strong brand that positions it as the “elastic” data platform, allowing it to command premium pricing versus traditional on‑premise solutions.

BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Snowflake 提供雲端資料倉儲平台，讓企業可以即時儲存、查詢與分享海量資料，且不需要自行建置硬體。它的護城河來自網路效應：客戶越多，平台的資料市集與整合工具就越豐富，使新進者難以提供同等廣度。搬遷 PB 級資料並重新編寫分析流程既費時又有風險，形成高切換成本。Snowflake 也具備強勢品牌，能以相對傳統本地解決方案的溢價定價出售服務。
