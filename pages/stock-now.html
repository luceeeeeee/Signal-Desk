BUSINESS OVERVIEW

ServiceNow (NOW) builds cloud‑based workflow software that lets large enterprises digit‑alise routine tasks such as IT help‑desk tickets, HR requests, and customer service cases.

Its moat comes from deep integration: once a company wires its internal processes into ServiceNow’s platform, switching to another vendor means re‑building dozens of custom workflows, which is costly and disruptive. The firm now serves roughly 20% of Fortune 500 companies, and its platform handles over a billion transactions each year, giving it a strong network effect and high switching costs.

企業概況

ServiceNow（NOW）提供雲端工作流程軟體，協助大型企業把 IT 請求、HR 申請、客服案件等日常工作數位化。

護城河在於深度整合：一旦企業把內部流程綁在 ServiceNow 平台，要換掉供應商就必須重建數十項客製化流程，成本高且會中斷營運。公司目前服務約 20% 的財富 500 大企業，每年處理逾十億筆交易，形成強大的網路效應與高切換成本。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months ServiceNow posted Q4 FY2025 revenue of $3.77 billion, a 22% year‑over‑year increase, and lifted its full‑year guidance for total revenue to $15.5 billion. The company also announced a strategic partnership with a major AI‑chip maker to embed generative‑AI assistants directly into its workflow catalog, expected to roll out to customers in early FY2026.

最近動向

過去六個月，ServiceNow 公布 2025 財年第四季營收 37.7 億美元，較去年同期成長 22%，並將全年度營收指引上調至 155 億美元。公司同時宣布與大型 AI 晶片製造商結成策略夥伴，於 2026 財年初在工作流程目錄中嵌入生成式 AI 助理。

CATALYSTS

  • AI‑augmented workflow launch: Embedding generative‑AI assistants will let users create and resolve tickets by natural language, expanding the addressable market and potentially adding 5‑7% revenue growth each year. Timing: beta in Q1 FY2026, full rollout Q3 FY2026.
  • International expansion: A new data‑center region in APAC opened in Q2 FY2026, giving local compliance and lower latency for Japanese and Australian customers, expected to drive 3‑4% incremental revenue in FY2027.
  • Cross‑sell of IT OM and HR SM suites: The company’s recent pricing bundle encourages existing IT customers to add HR Service Delivery, historically raising average contract value by ~12%. The effect should materialize over the next 12‑18 months.

催化劑

  • 【AI 強化工作流程上線】：將生成式 AI 助理嵌入平台，讓使用者可用自然語言建立與處理工單，將開拓可新增 5‑7% 年營收成長的市場。時程：2026 財年第一季測試版、第三季全面上線。
  • 【國際版圖擴張】：於 2026 財年第二季在亞太新設資料中心，提供日本與澳洲客戶合規與低延遲服務，預計 2027 財年帶來 3‑4% 營收增長。
  • 【IT OM 與 HR SM 捆綁銷售】：新價格組合鼓勵現有 IT 客戶同時採用 HR 服務，過往提升合約平均價值約 12%，效果將於未來 12‑18 個月顯現。

RISKS

  • Economic slowdown: A prolonged recession could force enterprise IT budgets to tighten, delaying new workflow automation projects and cutting renewal rates. A 5% drop in revenue would push the forward P/E below 15, making the stock vulnerable to a 10‑12% price decline.
  • Competitive encroachment: Large cloud players (e.g., Microsoft with Dynamics 365) are accelerating AI integration; if they win a few marquee enterprise contracts, ServiceNow could see a slowdown in new logo wins and margin compression.
  • Implementation complexity: The AI‑augmented workflow requires significant customer training and customization. If adoption lags, the expected 5‑7% revenue boost could be halved, dampening FY2027 guidance.

風險

  • 【經濟衰退】：若企業 IT 預算持續縮減，將推遲新工作流程自動化專案與續約率下降。營收若下降 5% 會使遠期本益比跌至 15 以下，股價可能承受 10‑12% 回檔。
  • 【競爭者侵蝕】：大型雲端巨頭（如微軟的 Dynamics 365）正加速 AI 整合；若搶下幾個關鍵企業合約，ServiceNow 可能失去新客戶開發速度，且毛利率受壓。
  • 【導入複雜度】：AI 強化工作流程需要大量客戶培訓與客製化，若採用速度不及預期，原本 5‑7% 的營收提升可能只有一半，將削弱 2027 財年的指引。

CONCLUSION

Buy at today’s $88.31 level. The forward P/E of 17.7 is well below the 5‑year historical average of ~24, giving a clear margin of safety, and the AI‑augmented workflow plus APAC rollout provide tangible upside of 8‑10% over the next two years. A breach below $80 would force a re‑evaluation, as it would imply slower growth or higher risk. The moat—high switching costs, extensive enterprise integration, and growing network effects—should remain robust for at least the next five years.

結論

在當前 88.31 美元的價格下建議買入。遠期本益比 17.7 明顯低於過去五年約 24 倍的平均值，安全邊際充足；AI 強化工作流程與亞太擴張將在未來兩年帶來約 8‑10% 的營收上行。若股價跌破 80 美元，需重新檢視，因可能暗示增長放緩或風險上升。高切換成本、深度企業整合與網路效應的護城河，預計在未來五年內仍具耐久性。
