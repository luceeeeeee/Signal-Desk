BUSINESS OVERVIEW

ServiceNow (NOW) builds cloud‑based workflow software that lets large enterprises digit‑alise routine tasks such as IT help‑desk tickets, HR requests, and customer service cases.

Its moat comes from deep integration: once a company wires its internal processes into ServiceNow’s platform, switching to another vendor means re‑building dozens of custom workflows, which is costly and disruptive. The firm now serves roughly 20% of Fortune 500 companies, and its platform handles over a billion transactions each year, giving it a strong network effect and high switching costs.

ServiceNow（NOW）提供雲端工作流程軟體，協助大型企業把 IT 請求、HR 申請、客服案件等日常工作數位化。

護城河在於深度整合：一旦企業把內部流程綁在 ServiceNow 平台，要換掉供應商就必須重建數十項客製化流程，成本高且會中斷營運。公司目前服務約 20% 的財富 500 大企業，每年處理逾十億筆交易，形成強大的網路效應與高切換成本。

In the last six months ServiceNow posted Q4 FY2025 revenue of $3.77 billion, a 22% year‑over‑year increase, and lifted its full‑year guidance for total revenue to $15.5 billion. The company also announced a strategic partnership with a major AI‑chip maker to embed generative‑AI assistants directly into its workflow catalog, expected to roll out to customers in early FY2026.
