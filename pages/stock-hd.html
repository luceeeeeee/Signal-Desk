BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English: Home Depot operates the largest chain of home‑improvement stores in the United States, selling building materials, garden supplies, appliances and DIY tools to both professionals and everyday homeowners. It controls roughly 30 % of the U.S. home‑improvement market, a share that gives it buying power with suppliers and the ability to keep shelves stocked when smaller rivals run out. The moat comes from three concrete sources: a massive network of over 2,300 big‑box stores that create high switching costs for contractors who rely on one‑stop shopping, a trusted brand that commands a price premium of about 5‑7 % over generic competitors, and a sophisticated logistics platform that can deliver bulky items within a day, something new entrants cannot easily replicate.

繁體中文：Home Depot是美國最大的家居改善連鎖店，銷售建材、園藝用品、家電與DIY工具，客群包括專業承包商與一般住宅主。它佔美國家居改善市場約30 %，這樣的規模讓它在供應商面前有議價能力，也能在小型競爭者缺貨時快速補貨。護城河來自三個具體因素：超過2,300家的大型門市網絡，使承包商習慣一次性採購，轉換成本高；受信賴的品牌能比普通商品多收5‑7 %的價格；以及先進的物流系統，能在一天內送達大件貨物，這是新競爭者難以快速建立的。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

English: In the latest quarter (January 2026) revenue fell 3.8 % year‑over‑year to $38.20 billion and net income dropped 28 % to $2.57 billion, reflecting weaker consumer spending on discretionary home projects. The company announced a $2 billion share‑repurchase program and accelerated its rollout of “HD Pro +” subscription services aimed at professional contractors, targeting an additional $1 billion in annual spend from that segment.

繁體中文：最新一季（2026年1月）營收同比下降3.8 %，至382億美元，淨利減少28 %，降至25.7億美元，顯示消費者在非必需家居工程上的支出減弱。公司宣布啟動20億美元的股票回購計畫，並加速推出針對專業承包商的 “HD Pro +” 訂閱服務，目標額外吸引約100億美元的年支出。

CATALYSTS

English:

  • Pro‑Level Subscription Expansion: The HD Pro + program locks contractors into a yearly fee for bulk‑discount pricing, faster checkout and exclusive inventory. If adoption reaches 15 % of the professional customer base (about 400,000 users), incremental revenue could add $600 million annually, likely boosting earnings in FY 2027.
  • Supply‑Chain Automation Rollout: Home Depot’s investment in warehouse robotics and AI‑driven inventory forecasting is slated for completion by Q3 2027. The technology promises a 2‑3 % lift in gross margin by reducing shrinkage and labor costs.
  • Housing‑Starts Rebound: The National Association of Home Builders projects U.S. housing starts to rise 6 % YoY in 2025. More new homes drive demand for building materials, and Home Depot historically captures about 10 % of that spend, translating to roughly $1 billion of incremental revenue.

  • 【專業訂閱服務擴張】：HD Pro + 以年費方式提供大量折扣、快速結帳及專屬庫存，鎖定承包商。若使用率達到專業客戶的15 %（約40萬人），每年可額外帶來6億美元營收，預計在2027財年提升獲利。
  • 【供應鏈自動化導入】：Home Depot在倉儲機器人與 AI 庫存預測的投資預計於2027年第三季完成。此技術可降低損耗與人工成本，提升毛利率2‑3 %。
