BUSINESS OVERVIEW

English:

Advanced Micro Devices (AMD) designs and sells central‑processing chips for computers, graphics cards for gaming and professional workstations, and data‑center processors that power cloud servers. Its moat comes from three hard‑to‑copy strengths: (1) a winning 7‑nanometer and 5‑nanometer process that only a few rivals can manufacture, (2) a deep software ecosystem (ROCm, drivers, and development tools) that makes it costly for customers to switch to another chip, and (3) a brand that now commands roughly a 40% price premium over the lowest‑cost Intel alternative in the high‑performance market.

繁體中文：

Advanced Micro Devices（AMD）設計並銷售電腦用的中央處理器、遊戲與專業工作站的顯示卡晶片，以及為雲端伺服器提供動能的資料中心處理器。它的護城河由三個難以模仿的優勢組成：（1）只有少數競爭者能做到的 7 nm 與 5 nm 製程技術；（2）深厚的軟體生態系統（ROCm、驅動程式與開發工具），使客戶換廠成本高；（3）品牌在高效能市場上能取得約 40% 的價格溢價，遠高於最低成本的 Intel 方案。
