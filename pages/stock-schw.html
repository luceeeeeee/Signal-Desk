BUSINESS OVERVIEW

Charles Schwab runs a large, low‑cost brokerage and wealth‑management platform that lets individuals and small institutions trade stocks, ETFs, and other securities online. It also offers banking, retirement accounts, and advisory services, all under one digital roof. Its moat comes from a massive scale of $84 billion in cash assets, a brand trusted by millions, and high switching costs because customers keep their cash, positions, and retirement plans linked to Schwab’s integrated platform. Roughly 25 % of U.S. retail brokerage accounts are with Schwab, giving it a clear market foothold.

Charles Schwab 主要提供低成本的線上券商與財富管理平台，讓個人與小型機構交易股票、ETF 等證券，並提供銀行、退休帳戶與投顧服務，全部集中在同一數位系統。 其護城河來自 840 億美元的龐大客戶資金規模、備受信賴的品牌，以及客戶把現金、持倉與退休金全都綁在 Schwab 生態系統內，形成高換戶成本。 約 25% 的美國散戶券商帳戶屬於 Schwab，確保其市場地位。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months Schwab’s net income rose to $2.46 billion in Q4 2025, a 7 % increase versus the prior quarter, driven by higher trading volume and fee‑based advisory revenue. The firm’s cash‑to‑debt ratio improved to 2.6 ×, reflecting strong deposit inflows and disciplined balance‑sheet management.

在過去六個月，Schwab 2025 年底季的淨利潤攀升至 24.6 億美元，較上一季增長 7%，主因是交易量上升與顧問費收入提升。 現金對負債比率提升至 2.6 倍，顯示存款流入強勁且資產負債表管理審慎。
