BUSINESS OVERVIEW 商業概況

Lockheed Martin designs, builds and services advanced aircraft, missiles, space systems and cyber‑defense solutions for governments worldwide, with the U.S. Department of Defense accounting for more than 70% of its sales. Its moat is the massive, decades‑long relationship network with the Pentagon, which creates switching costs so high that a rival would need to win multiple multi‑billion‑dollar contracts to make a dent. The company also holds a portfolio of patents and proprietary technologies that few competitors can replicate, and its brand is synonymous with “the most reliable fighter jet,” allowing it to charge a price premium on many programs.

Lockheed Martin 設計、製造及維護先進飛機、導彈、太空系統與資安防禦方案，主要客戶是各國政府，尤其是美國國防部佔超過 70% 銷售額。其護城河來自與五角大樓長達數十年的合作關係，任何競爭者若想奪取市場必須贏得多項數十億美元的合約，換供成本極高。公司另有大量專利與專有技術，且品牌等同於「最可靠的戰鬥機」，因此能在多項計畫上收取溢價。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS 最近動向

In the last six months Lockheed Martin secured three major contracts: a $17 billion F‑35 production deal, a $5 billion missile‑defense upgrade for the U.K., and a $9 billion space‑launch services agreement with the U.S. Air Force, pushing quarterly revenue to $18.02 billion in March 2026. At the same time, earnings fell 11.5% year‑over‑year because higher R&D spending and cost‑plus contracts squeezed net profit.

過去六個月，Lockheed Martin 獲得三筆重大合約：170 億美元 F‑35 產量合約、50 億美元英國導彈防禦升級、以及 90 億美元美國空軍太空發射服務，讓 2026 年 3 月的季營收升至 180.2 億美元。同期因研發支出上升與成本加成合約，盈餘較去年同期下滑 11.5%。

CATALYSTS 催化劑

  • FY 2026 Defense Budget Spike: The U.S. Congress is expected to approve a 4% increase in the defense budget by FY 2026, directly feeding new orders for next‑gen fighters and missile systems. Timing: FY 2026 (starts Oct 2025). Potential magnitude: $12‑$15 billion incremental revenue.
  • International F‑35 Export Ramp‑up: Several NATO allies (Poland, Finland, and Norway) are slated to sign F‑35 purchase agreements in the next 12‑18 months, which could add $8‑$10 billion in sales.
  • Space‑Launch Commercialization: Lockheed’s newly formed space‑launch subsidiary aims to certify its reusable launch vehicle by Q3 2026, opening a commercial market that could generate $3‑$5 billion annually after 2027.
  • 【財政年度 2026 國防預算激增】：美國國會預計在 2026 財政年度將國防預算提高 4%，直接推動下一代戰鬥機與導彈系統的新訂單。時間點：2026 財年（2025 年 10 月開始），潛在規模：120‑150 億美元的額外營收。
  • 【國際 F‑35 出口加速】：波蘭、芬蘭與挪威等北約盟友預計在未來 12‑18 個月內簽署 F‑35 採購合約，可能再帶來 80‑100 億美元的銷售額。
  • 【太空發射商業化】：Lockheed 的新成立太空發射子公司目標在 2026 年第三季完成可重複使用發射載具認證，2027 年起可開啟每年 30‑50 億美元的商業市場。

RISKS 風險

  • Program Delays & Cost Overruns: Major platforms like the F‑35 and the new hypersonic missile have a history of schedule slips; a six‑month delay could cut quarterly revenue by $1‑$2 billion and dent profit margins.
  • Geopolitical Budget Cuts: If a prolonged U.S. political stalemate forces a 2% reduction in the defense budget, annual contract wins could shrink by $5‑$7 billion, pressuring earnings.
  • Supply‑Chain Concentration: Over 70% of critical components come from three Tier‑1 suppliers; a disruption (e.g., labor strike or cyber‑attack) could halt production and force a quarterly earnings miss.
  • 【計畫延遲與成本超支】：F‑35 與新型高超音速導彈等關鍵平台常有進度拖延；若延遲六個月，季營收可能減少 10‑20 億美元，且毛利率受壓。
  • 【地緣政治預算削減】：若美國政治僵局導致國防預算縮減 2%，每年新合約可能減少 50‑70 億美元，直接沖擊盈餘。
  • 【供應鏈集中風險】：超過 70% 的關鍵零件來自三家一級供應商，任何供應中斷（如罷工、資安攻擊）都可能停產，導致季盈餘不及預期。

Buy at today’s price of $517.97. The forward P/E of 16.1 is well below the 5‑year average of ~19, indicating a cheap entry point for a company with a durable government‑backed moat.
