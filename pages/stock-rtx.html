BUSINESS OVERVIEW

RTX designs, manufactures and services advanced aerospace and defense systems, including fighter jets, missiles, radars, and satellite communications.

Its moat comes from deep integration in government defence contracts, high switching costs for the U.S. and allied militaries, and a portfolio of patented radar and propulsion technologies that few rivals can copy.

業務概覽

RTX 設計、製造並維護先進的航空與防禦系統，包含戰鬥機、導彈、雷達與衛星通訊。

護城河來源於與美國及盟國軍方的深度合約、軍方更換供應商成本極高，以及多項專利的雷達與推進技術，競爭者難以復製。

RECENT DEVELOPMENTS

In the last six months RTX landed three new multi‑year contracts worth roughly $4 billion, pushing Q1‑26 revenue up 8.7% YoY.

The company also announced a $1.2 billion cost‑saving program that will lower operating expenses by about 5% over the next two years.

最近發展

過去六個月，RTX 獲得三份總額約 40 億美元的多年合約，帶動 2026 年第一季營收同比成長 8.7%。

公司同時宣佈一項 12 億美元的降本計畫，預計未來兩年營運費用降低約 5%。

CATALYSTS

  • 2026 Defense Budget Cycle: The U.S. FY‑2026 defence budget is set to increase 4%; RTX is positioned to win a large share of new fighter‑jet and missile spend, potentially adding $3‑5 billion of top‑line revenue in the next 12‑18 months.
  • Airbus‑Raytheon Merger Completion: Final regulatory approval expected Q3‑2026 could create cross‑selling opportunities and cost synergies worth $800 million per year, boosting earnings.
  • Commercial Space Services Launch: RTX’s satellite‑bus division plans to start selling commercial launch services in 2027; early contracts could generate $200 million of recurring cash flow by 2028.

催化劑

  • 【2026 年防務預算週期】：美國 2026 財政年度防務預算將提升 4%；RTX 有望在新戰鬥機與導彈採購中奪得大量份額，未來 12‑18 個月可能為營收再添 30‑50 億美元。
  • 【Airbus‑Raytheon 合併完成】：預計 2026 年第三季取得最終批准，將帶來每年 8 億美元的成本協同效應，提升獲利。
  • 【商業太空服務推出】：RTX 衛星平台部門計畫於 2027 年開始提供商業發射服務，預估 2028 年可產生 2 億美元的持續營收。

RISKS

  • Government Spending Cuts: A delayed or reduced U.S. defence budget would shave 2‑3 billion dollars off projected revenue, pressuring earnings.
  • Integration Risk: The Airbus‑Raytheon merger could face cultural clashes or antitrust hurdles, delaying cost‑saving benefits and causing one‑time charges.
  • Supply‑Chain Constraints: Ongoing semiconductor shortages could push back delivery dates for key avionics, harming contract performance and margin.

風險

  • 【政府支出削減】：若美國防務預算延遲或縮減，將使預期營收減少 20‑30 億美元，壓縮獲利。
  • 【整合風險】：Airbus‑Raytheon 合併可能遭遇文化衝突或反托拉斯審查，導致成本協同延後並產生一次性費用。
  • 【供應鏈限制】：持續的半導體短缺可能拖延關鍵航空電子交付，影響合約執行與毛利率。

Buy at today’s $176 price. The forward P/E of 23.3 is slightly below the 5‑year average of 25, and the new contracts plus cost‑saving program give clear upside.
